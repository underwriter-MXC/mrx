<section class="container section" aria-labelledby="methodology-plain-english">
  <Heading id="methodology-plain-english" level={2} size="3xl">
    The approach, in plain English
  </Heading>
  <Text class="text--lead" style="margin-top: var(--space-3); max-width: 64ch;">
    A discounted-cash-flow review models expected future royalty income from the section, applies a
    discount rate that reflects the risk of the cash flows, and arrives at a present-value range.
    The range is the directional assessment; the assumptions and the inputs are what make it useful.
  </Text>

  <ol class="process-steps" style="margin-top: var(--space-8);" aria-label="DCF review steps">
    <li class="process-step">
      <span class="process-step__title">Pull production</span>
      <span class="process-step__text">Recent and historical royalty income anchors the review when records are available.</span>
    </li>
    <li class="process-step">
      <span class="process-step__title">Model decline</span>
      <span class="process-step__text">Decline-curve context helps frame expected future cash flow.</span>
    </li>
    <li class="process-step">
      <span class="process-step__title">Apply discount rate</span>
      <span class="process-step__text">Operator, basin, title, and data confidence shape the risk-adjusted rate.</span>
    </li>
    <li class="process-step">
      <span class="process-step__title">Layer offer terms</span>
      <span class="process-step__text">If you have an offer, clauses and conveyed rights are reviewed separately.</span>
    </li>
    <li class="process-step">
      <span class="process-step__title">State the range</span>
      <span class="process-step__text">The written review states the range and the assumptions behind it.</span>
    </li>
  </ol>
</section>
