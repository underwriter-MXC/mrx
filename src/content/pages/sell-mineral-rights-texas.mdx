    answer: 'Texas real-property instruments are generally recorded with the county clerk for the county where the property is located. The correct county and legal description should be confirmed before relying on a record search.'

    answer: 'RRC resources can provide well, lease, operator, permit, and production context. Those records do not by themselves establish private mineral ownership or settle the meaning of a deed, lease, or purchase agreement.'

    answer: 'A deed may convey a defined fraction, tract, depth, or other portion while reserving the balance. The language must describe the intended transfer accurately and should be reviewed by a qualified Texas attorney.'
