faq:
  - question: 'Is this an appraisal?'
    answer: 'No. Our review is a directional underwriter assessment, not a certified valuation. We use a DCF approach when the records support it, but the output is a range with the assumptions stated. Owners often consider hiring a state-licensed or otherwise qualified appraiser when a certified value is required, such as for certain estate or tax filings.'
  - question: 'Is this legal or tax guidance?'
    answer: 'No. We are not your attorney or your CPA. We can help you think through questions worth asking an attorney or tax professional qualified for the applicable state, and we recommend verifying ownership, royalty, tax, and production assumptions before any transaction.'
  - question: 'Will you pressure me to sell?'
    answer: 'No. The review is genuinely free, with no obligation to sell. If you decide to hold, we still consider the conversation a good use of our time. We do not run follow-up pressure campaigns or push you toward a decision.'
  - question: 'What if I already have an offer?'
    answer: 'That is the right moment for a second look. Bring the offer document, royalty statements, and any lease or division-order paperwork. We will walk through what the offer assumes, what it may be leaving out, and the questions worth asking before you sign.'
  - question: 'Is it really free?'
    answer: 'Yes. The intake form does not collect a card. We do not charge a fee to review your information. If a transaction results from a review and we become a buyer, we disclose that relationship in writing before any agreement is signed; the price is then part of the agreement, not the review.'
  - question: 'What documents do I need?'
    answer: 'A recent royalty check stub, a copy of any offer letter you have received, and a lease or division order if you have one. If you do not have them, we will help you figure out what to ask for; many county clerk and operator records are available online or by request.'
  - question: 'How long does the review take?'
    answer: 'Timing varies based on document availability, title complexity, the number of counties or operators involved, and the questions that arise during review. We will keep you informed as the review progresses.'
