> **Source-preparation boundary.** This blank map organizes controlled source locations for one already-structured exchange file. It does not determine whether an exchange qualifies, select a taxpayer or answer, judge a deadline, calculate an amount, choose a form line or attachment, complete Form 8824, design or cure a transaction, or provide owner-specific tax or legal guidance. Keep names, TINs, addresses, property descriptions, dates, amounts, agreements, closing records, and workpapers in an authorized controlled system. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

An **IRS Form 8824 source documents** map can help an owner and an authorized tax professional see where the exchange file's records are stored. It is a preparation index, not a tax worksheet and not a substitute for the form instructions.

Use the map only after the exchange structure and professional team already exist. If the owner still needs to decide whether Section 1031 may apply, start with the general [1031 eligibility and process guide](/blog/1031-exchange-for-mineral-rights-does-it-qualify-and-how-does-it-work/) and contact a qualified tax professional, attorney, and qualified intermediary before a transfer.

## Direct answer

Create one controlled map ID for one exchange file. For each current Form 8824 request category, record the location of an existing source package, its access boundary, a source limitation, and the professional responsible for review. Use one of three outcomes: `mapped`, `incomplete`, or `professional-review-required`.

Do not copy an owner's actual name, identifying number, property description, date, related-party answer, amount, liability, basis, gain, recapture item, or other return value into the reusable public map. Do not decide which source controls or what answer belongs on the form. The authorized tax professional must connect the complete evidence to the current instructions and the owner's return.

## Start with the current official request categories

The current official [IRS Form 8824](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8824.pdf) is labeled for 2025. Under the Part I heading, requested categories cover property descriptions and acquisition, transfer, written-identification, and receipt dates. A separate question addresses whether the exchange was made with a related party, directly or indirectly.

Part II contains related-party identity, relationship, disposition, and exception questions. Part III contains the form's consideration, property, liability, basis, realized-gain, recognized-gain, deferred-gain, and replacement-basis calculations.

Those labels define source categories for this map. They do not tell this workflow whether the owner's facts belong in a category, which answer is correct, or how an amount should be calculated. The current [Instructions for Form 8824](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8824) remain the professional reference for the form's purpose and line-specific work.

## Use three controlled outcomes

Use exactly one outcome for each source category:

- **Mapped:** the location of a controlled record was identified.
- **Incomplete:** the expected record or source pointer was not located or supplied.
- **Professional-review-required:** the item requires characterization, calculation, selection, interpretation, reconciliation, deadline analysis, or filing judgment.

`Mapped` does not mean relevant, complete, accurate, qualifying, timely, reconciled, or ready to file. `Incomplete` does not prove that the underlying property, event, relationship, amount, agreement, liability, or workpaper does not exist. `Professional-review-required` is a routing state, not an adverse conclusion.

> **Outcome limitation: keep this beside the map.** This record shows only where a source package may be located and what remains unresolved. It does not establish taxpayer identity, property classification, eligibility, related-party treatment, deadline compliance, value, basis, gain, liability, expense, recapture, form accuracy, filing status, or legal effect.

## Protect the exchange file

Do not place a completed form, screenshot, taxpayer name, TIN, address, property identifier, legal description, date, amount, agreement excerpt, selected answer, or professional workpaper in the public article, image, URL, analytics, or search index.

Store the completed source map with the exchange file in an authorized controlled system. Use access-controlled pointers so the authorized professional can reach the source without copying protected content into public or general-purpose records. Record access and handoff according to the organization's privacy and retention rules.

## Stop at professional review

Close the preparation map and hand off immediately when the work requires:

- deciding whether the mineral-related interest or transaction qualifies;
- selecting the taxpayer, entity, property classification, related-party answer, date, value, or return year;
- applying identification, receipt, return-due-date, disaster-relief, or other timing rules;
- characterizing cash, other property, liabilities, expenses, basis, depreciation, or recapture;
- calculating any Form 8824 line or another return item;
- choosing an attachment, schedule, form, filing position, correction, or amendment;
- designing, changing, or attempting to cure an exchange structure;
- recommending or evaluating a qualified intermediary; or
- reaching an owner-specific tax, legal, accounting, title, valuation, investment, or transaction conclusion.

The authorized tax professional, attorney, and qualified intermediary must perform the work appropriate to their roles. This map does not attest that the file is complete, the exchange is compliant, or the return is ready.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a complete source map mean Form 8824 is ready to file?

No. A complete set of source pointers is still only a preparation index. The authorized tax professional must review the actual documents, resolve inconsistencies, select answers, perform calculations, and determine the filing package.

### Can the map decide which property description belongs on the form?

No. It can point to the controlling instrument package. Selecting or drafting the form description requires professional review of the exact property, instruments, transaction, and current instructions.

### What if two closing records show different dates or amounts?

Record the conflict as an unresolved item and preserve pointers to both controlled sources. Do not choose one, average amounts, infer a date, or silently overwrite a record.

### Should I calculate adjusted basis before sending the map to my preparer?

No. Point to the acquisition and adjustment records already retained. Use a professional-supplied basis workpaper only when the authorized professional provides it. Do not calculate or estimate basis inside this source map.

### Can this map confirm the 45-day or 180-day requirement was met?

No. It may point to written-identification and closing evidence, but it does not apply a deadline, determine the triggering date, account for a return due date or extension, or reach a compliance conclusion.
