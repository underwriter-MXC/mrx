> **Public retrieval boundary.** This article records one authorized manual attempt through the Texas Railroad Commission's Oil & Gas Imaged Records **Oil and Gas Potential** profile. It does not interpret a file or determine record completeness, well identity or status, permit, completion, plugging, production, reserves, safety, environmental condition, compliance, property connection, ownership, title, payment, acreage, value, offer quality, legal effect, tax, or transaction outcome. It does not provide legal, title, land, regulatory, environmental, geological, engineering, tax, accounting, appraisal, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC potential file retrieval** worksheet preserves how one manual source attempt was made. It identifies the official menu route, retains the displayed record-family and profile labels, points to controlled criteria, timestamps the attempt, preserves access, Potential-link, `FILM YR`, and viewer states, retains an evidence reference, attaches source-coverage notes, and assigns one bounded outcome.

Use only **located**, **not located**, or **unverified**. Those terms describe one retrieval attempt. They do not convert a menu label, file link, image, source value, microfilm note, or empty result into a conclusion about a well, property, owner, regulator action, valuation, offer, or transaction.

## Begin with the official Imaged Records entry route

Start with the Texas RRC [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory and preserve its official **Oil & Gas Imaged Records Menu** route. Record the directory URL, displayed source label, access time, and the menu URL before attempting to open any profile.

This is a one-attempt manual record, not an automated research workflow. Do not scrape the source, retrieve records in volume, create a repeated polling process, bypass an access control, handle a CAPTCHA through automation, or work around a validation, session, timeout, or viewer state. When the official path cannot be completed as authorized, document the stop point and use **unverified**.

The RRC [Imaged Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/imaged-records/) page separately lists **Oil & Gas Well Records (Potential files)**, Dry Hole Files, and Oil & Gas Well Logs. That separation matters. Record the family and profile that the official route actually displays; do not substitute a nearby collection because its search controls look familiar.

## Keep the Potential profile separate from adjacent sources

The official [Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) page names an **Oil and Gas Potential profile** and a separate **Well Log (WL) profile**. This worksheet owns only one manual Potential-profile attempt.

| Source route or family | Boundary for this worksheet |
|---|---|
| Oil and Gas Potential profile | Preserve one manual attempt, its displayed states, and its evidence trail. |
| Well Log profile | Separate imaged-record profile; do not merge log images or viewer conclusions. |
| Wellbore Query | Separate data-query system; do not copy its rows or status fields into this attempt. |
| Drilling Permit Query | Separate electronic permit route with its own coverage and result states. |
| Completions Query | Separate online packet route; do not treat a Potential link as a completion-query result. |
| P-4, P-17, Dry Hole, GIS, EDMS, hearing, or production sources | Separate record families or systems requiring separate attempt records. |

The profile name is a routing fact, not a property fact. It does not establish that every expected document is online, that an image belongs to a reader's mineral interest, that the filing is current or complete, or that a nearby electronic route will show the same material.

## Create the attempt header before entering criteria

Assign a neutral attempt ID that reveals no private property, party, ownership, or transaction information. Then record:

- the official Research Queries, Imaged Records, and menu URLs;
- the displayed record-family and Oil and Gas Potential profile labels;
- access date, clock time, and explicit timezone;
- the authorized manual access method;
- a controlled criteria-set reference rather than sensitive values;
- each source-displayed search-key category actually used;
- the displayed access, validation, submission, result, or interruption state;
- the Potential-link state and `FILM YR` state, if displayed;
- the viewer or image state, if any;
- the retained evidence location and access restriction;
- source coverage, later-route, and retrieval-date notes; and
- the transfer destination, recipient limits, and unresolved questions.

Do not publish API numbers, districts, field identifiers, lease names or numbers, well numbers, operator names, locations, date ranges, images, or party names merely because a source accepts or displays them. Keep necessary values in an authorized controlled location and let the worksheet point to that record.

## Record categories without asserting a connection

The official well-record pages describe searches by key fields or full text and list information that can help identify requested records. Preserve only the category labels actually used and the controlled criteria reference. Do not turn a search category into a representation that the criteria identify the correct private interest.

An accepted API, district, field, lease, well, operator, location, or date value does not prove a property connection. A source-displayed label does not establish the current operator, lease status, ownership, title, payment entitlement, acreage, or legal position. Those questions require separate evidence and qualified review.

## Preserve the Potential-link and FILM YR states

The [Well Records FAQs](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/about-us/faqs/oil-gas-faq/well-records-faqs/) state that the notation **FILM YR** beneath a Potential link indicates that images for that well record were taken from microfilm. If that notation is actually displayed, transcribe the label exactly, preserve the visible state, and link it to the attempt evidence.

Do not infer more than the source says. A `FILM YR` notation does not by itself prove when a document was filed, whether every page was filmed, whether the image set is complete, whether the file belongs to the intended well or interest, or whether a newer electronic record exists elsewhere. If the notation is absent, record it as **not displayed** rather than guessing.

The FAQ also supplies film-cycle and viewer context. Preserve only the source wording relevant to the displayed state. Do not reconstruct a historical record from a cycle note or treat a viewer problem as evidence that the underlying image is missing.

## Distinguish later electronic routes

The Oil and Gas Well Records page describes the Potential profile as including applications to drill, oil and gas completion reports, plugging reports, producer's transportation authority, and miscellaneous records from 1964 to the present. Treat that as source-described profile scope, not as a promise that every listed document will appear for every search.

The same page directs applications to drill submitted after May 2005 to the electronic Drilling Permit Query. The Imaged Records page and FAQ separately direct completion packets received after February 2011 to the Oil & Gas Completion Query. Preserve those statements as routing notes. Do not merge a Drilling Permit or Completions query into the Potential-profile attempt, and do not claim that the stated dates prove where a particular document must exist.

If the intended record category falls under a separate route, close or hold the Potential-profile attempt with its actual bounded outcome. Create a new authorized attempt record for the other source instead of silently changing systems under the same ID.

## Apply fail-closed outcome rules

Use the displayed state, not the desired answer, to select the outcome.

| Displayed attempt state | Bounded outcome | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Official menu or profile cannot be opened, including an HTTP block | **unverified** | Entry route, timestamp, displayed response, and stop point. |
| Validation failure, error, timeout, session termination, default screen, or unavailable viewer | **unverified** | Exact visible state, timestamp, criteria reference, and stop point. |
| Submission or accepted criteria cannot be confirmed | **unverified** | Category labels used, controlled criteria reference, and retained state evidence. |
| Accepted criteria produce a completed empty result attributable to this attempt | **not located** | Completed state, timestamp, criteria reference, and evidence reference. |
| Accepted criteria display responsive Potential-profile material | **located** | Result or link state, timestamp, criteria reference, viewer state, and evidence reference. |

Do not retry until a preferred outcome appears. If an authorized correction is required, create a new attempt ID or a clearly linked correction event. Preserve the original state rather than overwriting it.

**Not located** is deliberately narrow. It does not mean a document never existed, is absent from all Commission records, is absent from a different profile, or has no bearing on some separate review. It means only that one completed attempt displayed an empty result attributable to its accepted criteria.

## Record image and viewer states without interpreting documents

If the source presents a link or image, record whether it was displayed, whether the authorized viewer opened it, and where the retained evidence is stored. Use **unverified** when the image cannot be opened or the viewer state is unclear. Do not call an inaccessible image empty, corrupt, irrelevant, or absent unless the source itself establishes that state and the evidence preserves it.

A visible page remains retrieved source material. This worksheet does not determine whether a displayed item is an application, completion report, plugging report, P-4, plat, survey, correspondence, or other document unless the source labels it and that label is preserved. It never interprets the contents as proof of a permit, completion, plugging, well status, production, compliance, environmental condition, property interest, ownership, or value.

The direct Neubus application was not queried for this article's identity decision. The article therefore makes no claim that a profile submission occurred, that a result was returned, or that a live application state was verified. Its factual support comes from the accessible official RRC pages named in the source list.

## Preserve coverage notes as limitations

For each attempt, copy the relevant source-described period, record category, later electronic-route statement, and access date into a coverage note. Keep the source URL beside the note so a later reviewer can recheck it.

Coverage language is not a completeness guarantee. It does not establish that every expected document was received, indexed, imaged, migrated, or made accessible. It also does not establish that a record outside the described period is absent from Commission holdings. If the requested period or document type appears outside the profile's stated scope, record the limitation and plan a separate authorized source check.

The Oil and Gas Well Records page also states that the RRC lacks authority over lease and royalty matters, financing or investment, property rights, and bankruptcy. That boundary reinforces the narrow purpose here. An RRC retrieval event cannot resolve private title, ownership, payment, value, legal, tax, or transaction questions.

## Prevent substantive inference during transfer

When responsive material is displayed, preserve the source path and evidence reference without promoting the event into a conclusion. A Potential-profile link or image does not establish:

- that the file is complete, current, correctly indexed, or the only responsive record;
- that it belongs to the reader's mineral or royalty interest;
- that an operator, field, lease, well, district, location, or API reference is current or correctly connected;
- that a permit was effective, a well was completed or plugged, or the filing was accepted as legally sufficient;
- that a well is active, inactive, producing, compliant, safe, or environmentally sound; or
- that the material affects ownership, title, payment, acreage, reserves, value, an offer, legal rights, tax, or a transaction.

Preserve the navigation chain as separate evidence:

`official RRC directory -> Imaged Records menu -> displayed Oil and Gas Potential profile -> controlled criteria reference -> displayed result or Potential-link state -> FILM YR and viewer states -> retained evidence reference`

Attach a transfer note that repeats the attempt ID, official route, timestamp and timezone, profile label, controlled criteria reference, displayed states, bounded outcome, evidence location, coverage limitations, unresolved questions, and recheck requirement. The next reviewer should never have to infer what **located**, **not located**, or **unverified** meant.

| Official entry route | Research Queries, Imaged Records, and menu URLs |
| Displayed source identity | Record-family and Oil and Gas Potential profile labels |
| Access timestamp | Date, time, and timezone |
| Authorized access method | One manual attempt only |
| Search-key categories used | Exact source-displayed category labels |
| Controlled criteria reference | Authorized location; do not expose unnecessary values |
| Access or submission state | Not started, blocked, validation, accepted, error, timeout, or interrupted |
| Displayed result state | Default, responsive, completed empty, Potential link, image route, or unclear |
| Potential-link state | Displayed, not displayed, inaccessible, or unclear |
| FILM YR state | Exact notation if displayed; otherwise not displayed or unclear |
| Viewer or image state | Not presented, presented, opened, unavailable, or unclear |
| Bounded outcome | Located, not located, or unverified |
| Retained evidence reference | Screenshot, note, controlled file, or other authorized reference |
| Coverage and route note | Source wording, period, later route, and access date |
| Adjacent routes excluded | Separate systems not searched under this attempt |
| Transfer note | Recipient, limits, unresolved questions, and recheck requirement |

## Final quality check

Before closing the record, confirm that:

- the attempt started from the official Imaged Records route and displayed the Oil and Gas Potential profile;
- the timestamp includes a timezone;
- source labels and used search-key categories are exact;
- actual criteria values remain controlled and are not unnecessarily published;
- blocked, error, timeout, validation, default, session, and viewer states remain **unverified**;
- **not located** is used only for a completed empty result attributable to accepted criteria;
- Potential-link, `FILM YR`, viewer, evidence, coverage, and transfer states are recorded separately;
- later Drilling Permit and Completions routes were noted but not merged;
- no automation, bulk retrieval, repeated-query workflow, bypass, CAPTCHA handling, or workaround occurred; and
- no record interpretation or well, property, ownership, production, value, offer, legal, tax, or transaction conclusion was added.

If any item fails, keep the attempt **unverified**. The worksheet succeeds when it preserves what happened, including an incomplete or inconclusive attempt, without expanding a retrieval event into a technical, property, or business conclusion.
