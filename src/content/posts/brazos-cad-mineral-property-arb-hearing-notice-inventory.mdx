> **Retained-notice inventory boundary.** Use this blank inventory only after an authorized person has received a written ARB hearing notice concerning an already-filed property-tax protest. It records exact visible fields and accompanying-material labels from one retained notice packet. It does not search for an account or status, route contact, assemble evidence, calculate an interval or deadline, select an appearance method, request a change, interpret the notice, determine validity or service, disclose private data, or predict a hearing or outcome. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Brazos CAD mineral property ARB hearing notice** inventory should preserve what one retained written notice contains without turning the document into an owner-specific conclusion. Record the exact source identity, tax year as displayed, notice date as displayed, hearing date, time, place and subject matter exactly as displayed, delivery channel, page count, visible enclosure labels, retained-copy reference, observation date, time and time zone, source limitation, discrepancy note, and handoff state.

Use only four document-workflow states: `inventoried`, `not located`, `conflict recorded`, and `authorized-human-stop`. These labels describe inventory work. They do not mean authentic, complete, valid, timely, served, scheduled, sufficient, accepted, or legally effective.

## Confirm the starting condition before inventory work

This workflow has three prerequisites:

1. An authorized person already filed the protest through an approved channel.
2. The authorized person received and retained a written item presented as the hearing notice.
3. The retained item and related private data are stored in an authorized controlled system.

If any prerequisite is unresolved, record `authorized-human-stop`. Do not use a public search result, email preview, calendar entry, phone note, or memory as a substitute for the retained source.

Keep adjacent tasks separate:

- Use the [Brazos CAD account-search worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-account-search-retrieval-provenance-worksheet/) for one official public account-search attempt.
- Use the [Brazos CAD contact-routing worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-industrial-account-contact-routing-worksheet/) to record a source-displayed destination before contact.
- Use the [Brazos County protest evidence packet](/blog/brazos-county-texas-mineral-rights-property-tax-protest-evidence-packet/) to index materials before submission.
- Use the [Brazos CAD protest-status log](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-property-protest-status-verification-log/) for a time-stamped observation of what a public source displays after submission.
- Use this inventory only for one retained written notice and the materials physically or digitally retained with it.

The inventory does not prove that an item is the controlling notice. It only makes the retained packet reproducible for authorized review.

## Create one inventory header for one retained packet

Assign one neutral inventory ID that reveals no owner, address, account, property, notice number, or hearing detail. Record the controlled storage location by reference instead of copying the private source into the reusable template.

The header should contain:

- **Inventory ID:** a neutral internal reference;
- **source as displayed:** the exact sender or publisher identity visible on the retained item;
- **source format:** paper, authorized electronic copy, or another exact observed format;
- **received-copy reference:** the controlled pointer to the retained source;
- **page count as retained:** the pages present in this specific copy;
- **observation timestamp:** the date, time, and time zone when the inventory was made;
- **source limitation:** any visible limitation plus the fact that inventory does not authenticate the item; and
- **workflow state:** one of the four allowed labels.

Do not enter personal or property data in a public copy. Names, mailing and email addresses, account numbers, property identifiers, legal descriptions, notice contents, dates, times, places, subject matter, delivery records, correspondence, and completed inventories belong only in the authorized system.

## Transcribe notice fields without normalizing them

The current [Texas Comptroller protest resource](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/protests/) explains that the written post-filing notice includes the hearing date, time, place, subject matter, and information about accessing estimated taxes. That official context supports a literal field inventory, not interpretation.

For every visible field, record three things together:

- the field label exactly as displayed;
- the field value exactly as displayed in the controlled record; and
- the page or section reference where it appears.

Preserve capitalization, punctuation, abbreviations, and displayed time-zone notation. Do not convert a date format, add a time zone, calculate elapsed days, infer whether delivery occurred, or rewrite the subject matter into a legal category. If a defined field is absent from the retained copy, mark it `not located`; do not supply a value from memory or another source.

The official [Texas Comptroller forms index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/forms/) currently identifies Form 50-216 as the Appraisal Review Board Protest Hearing Notice. Record that current source identity in the inventory evidence, but do not assume that every retained item must look identical to a model form or that matching a model authenticates an owner-specific notice.

## Inventory each accompanying item separately

Treat every page, attachment, insert, link label, or named accompanying item as its own inventory entry. Record only what is visibly present.

For each item, capture:

1. **Item ID.** Use a neutral sequence such as `ITEM-A`.
2. **Visible label.** Copy the title or label exactly into the controlled record.
3. **Format.** Record paper page, attachment, authorized link reference, or another observed format.
4. **Count.** Record the pages or discrete files actually retained.
5. **Relationship.** State `retained with notice`, `referenced by notice`, or `relationship unresolved`.
6. **Controlled reference.** Point to the authorized copy without exposing it publicly.
7. **Observation.** Choose `inventoried`, `not located`, `conflict recorded`, or `authorized-human-stop`.

Do not mark a packet complete merely because the page count matches a printed number or an enclosure label appears. The inventory records presence and absence in one retained copy. It does not decide whether an omitted item was required, whether a referenced link was delivered, or whether any material is legally sufficient.

## Use the four states literally

### Inventoried

`Inventoried` means the defined field or item was visibly present and recorded with its exact source reference. It does not authenticate the source or establish completeness, service, scheduling, validity, or legal effect.

### Not located

`Not located` means the defined field or referenced item was not found in the specific retained packet during the recorded review. It does not prove that the item was never sent, does not exist, or is legally required.

### Conflict recorded

`Conflict recorded` means two retained items show different text for the same defined field, or a visible reference does not align with the material retained under that label. Preserve both source references. Do not choose which one controls or explain why they differ.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before credentials, contact, upload, submission, reschedule or change request, signature, payment, private-data disclosure, deadline calculation, legal interpretation, tax guidance from a qualified professional, appraisal judgment, valuation, representation, or strategy.

The workflow ends at the stop. It does not draft an email, place a call, click a link, enter a portal, choose an attendance method, request different scheduling, or submit anything.

## Separate the retained notice from public status fields

The current [Brazos CAD Property Search](https://esearch.brazoscad.org/) exposes public protest-related labels and states that its information is for research and that legal descriptions and acreage should be verified before legal use. Those limits matter, but this inventory performs no public search.

Do not copy a date or phrase from the public display into the retained-notice field merely to resolve a blank or difference. If an authorized reviewer has both sources, preserve each independently:

- source A: the retained written notice;
- source B: the separate time-stamped public observation;
- exact field text from each source;
- source-specific tax year or covered period;
- source-specific observation time; and
- `conflict recorded` when the defined text differs.

The [separate status-verification log](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-property-protest-status-verification-log/) owns source B. This page owns source A. Keeping them separate protects provenance and prevents a later observation from silently changing the retained notice record.

## Preserve institutional context without deciding legal effect

The [Texas Comptroller ARB page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/arb/) supplies current statewide context for the appraisal review board and the hearing process. The Comptroller protest resource also states that its office cannot advise parties about a matter under protest. The inventory therefore records the source and routes interpretation elsewhere; it does not speak for the ARB.

Texas Tax Code [Chapter 41](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docs/tx/pdf/tx.41.pdf) supplies current statutory notice and hearing context. Citing the chapter does not interpret it. Do not use this inventory to determine whether notice was properly delivered, whether timing requirements were met, whether an appearance option is available, whether a right was waived, whether the subject matter is correct, or what remedy may apply.

Those questions require current official materials, the owner-specific record, and qualified review. Preserve the question in neutral language and stop.

## Record discrepancies without recommending action

A discrepancy note should identify only the observable difference:

- `The retained notice and referenced item use different field text.`
- `The defined enclosure label is visible, but the item was not located in this retained packet.`
- `Two retained pages display different times.`
- `The displayed tax years differ between the retained sources.`
- `The next review would require private data or owner-specific interpretation.`

Avoid conclusions such as:

- `The notice is invalid.`
- `The district missed the deadline.`
- `The owner must request another date.`
- `The hearing method was selected incorrectly.`
- `The appraisal will be reduced.`
- `The owner should contact the ARB immediately.`

The observable difference belongs in the inventory. Its cause, importance, legal effect, and next action do not.

## Prepare a controlled handoff packet

When review is needed, the handoff packet can include:

- neutral inventory ID;
- exact displayed source identity;
- controlled retained-copy reference;
- notice field labels and controlled values;
- page and item inventory;
- observation date, time, and time zone;
- exact discrepancy note;
- source limitations;
- one allowed workflow state; and
- one unresolved question.

A neutral question might be: `What current official source or qualified review is required to understand the recorded difference?` Do not convert the handoff into a request, argument, deadline opinion, or recommendation.

Before disclosure, an authorized human should verify the recipient, role, purpose, minimum necessary data, approved channel, and current official instructions. The reusable public template should never contain the completed packet.

## Final verification checklist

Before closing the inventory, confirm that:

- the protest was already filed through an authorized process;
- an authorized person received and retained the written notice;
- one neutral inventory ID maps to one retained packet;
- the source identity and format are copied exactly;
- the tax year and notice fields are transcribed as displayed;
- dates, times, places, and subject matter remain in the controlled system;
- no interval, deadline, or time-zone conversion was calculated;
- every retained or referenced accompanying item has a separate entry;
- absent items are marked `not located` without a legal conclusion;
- differences are preserved as `conflict recorded` without choosing a controlling source;
- no appearance method, contact, change request, evidence step, or strategy is recommended;
- private records are not copied into the public template; and
- interpretation or owner-specific action ends at `authorized-human-stop`.

Forms, systems, field labels, and procedures can change. Re-check the current official source when the inventory is used. Do not substitute a search snippet, old model form, prior-year packet, another owner's notice, or a public status display for the retained owner-authorized source.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does inventoried mean the notice is valid?

No. It means only that the defined visible field or accompanying item was recorded from the retained source. It does not establish authenticity, completeness, validity, service, scheduling, timeliness, or legal effect.

### Can the inventory calculate the time between displayed dates?

No. Copy each displayed date and time exactly into the controlled record, but do not calculate an interval or deadline. Use current official materials and qualified advice for owner-specific timing questions.

### Can this page choose an appearance method?

No. It may record a visible instruction or enclosure label, but it does not select a method, make a request, or recommend action.

### What if a referenced enclosure is missing?

Record the exact reference, mark the item `not located`, preserve the observation time, and use `authorized-human-stop`. Do not infer whether the packet is defective or what action is required.

### Can the reusable worksheet contain owner details?

No. Keep identifiers, dates, times, places, subject matter, correspondence, and completed inventories in an authorized controlled system. Use neutral references in the blank public inventory.
