> **Retained-form source-scope boundary.** This blank worksheet documents one controlled capture from one already-retained Form 1099-MISC. It does not request or retrieve a form, contact a payer, authenticate the source, verify a payer or recipient, reconcile a payment, determine tax treatment, prepare or amend a return, or provide owner-specific legal or tax guidance. Keep completed forms, screenshots, names, addresses, TINs, account numbers, amounts, and completed worksheets in an authorized controlled system. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

An **IRS Form 1099-MISC royalty Box 2** worksheet can preserve where a displayed royalty field came from without turning that display into a tax conclusion. Use it for one form already in the authorized record, one source-capture event, and one bounded outcome.

This workflow does not interpret a royalty statement, match a deposit, calculate depletion, or decide how a return should be filed. Use the broader [retained-evidence source-scope worksheet](/blog/mineral-rights-retained-evidence-source-scope-worksheet/) when the artifact is not specifically a Form 1099-MISC Box 2 record.

## Direct answer

Create one worksheet ID for one already-retained Form 1099-MISC. Preserve the exact form title and revision displayed on that source, the calendar-year field, the copy designation, and the correction state. Record a VOID state only when that label appears on the retained copy.

Use controlled pointers for the payer block, recipient block, TIN fields, FATCA checkbox, account-number field, Box 2 `Royalties` field, Box 4 `Federal income tax withheld` field, and Boxes 16 through 18 state-information fields when those labels appear. Add one controlled retained-evidence pointer, one source limitation, and one outcome: `captured`, `incomplete`, or `unverified`.

Stop there. Do not put an actual name, address, telephone number, TIN, account number, dollar amount, calendar-year result, completed checkbox state, screenshot, or completed form into the public template, image, URL, analytics, or search index.

## Start with the retained copy, not a generic form memory

The current official [IRS Form 1099-MISC PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1099msc.pdf) is labeled `Rev. December 2026`. Its copies display a `For calendar year` field, payer and recipient blocks, TIN fields, a FATCA checkbox, an account-number field, Box 2 `Royalties`, Box 4 `Federal income tax withheld`, and Boxes 16 through 18 for state information.

Copy presentation is not identical. Copy A and Copy 1 display `VOID` and `CORRECTED`; the recipient copies display a correction state without the same VOID presentation. That difference is why this worksheet starts with the retained copy itself. Do not add `VOID`, a copy designation, a revision, or another field just because it appears on a different official copy.

The current [IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec) describe the December 2026 revision as a continuous-use revision for calendar year 2026 and later years until the IRS issues a superseding revision. They also provide specific instructions for Box 2. Preserve the retained source's actual revision and year display; do not assign the current revision to an older form.

## Preserve only adjacent fields that are actually shown

Box 2 is the anchor for this worksheet. The official instructions identify that box as `Royalties` and describe its reporting scope. This article does not apply those reporting rules to an owner. It uses the official label only to prevent a source-capture record from silently confusing Box 2 with another field.

Adjacent controlled pointers may include Box 4 and Boxes 16 through 18 because the current official form displays those fields. They are not calculations or instructions. A displayed Box 4 value does not tell this workflow whether withholding was required, correctly computed, paid, credited, or reportable. State fields do not decide state residence, sourcing, filing duty, credit, allocation, or tax due.

The account-number and FATCA fields also remain pointers only. The IRS instructions provide context for those fields, but this worksheet does not decide whether either field was required, correctly completed, or applicable to the recipient.

## Use three controlled outcomes

Use exactly one outcome:

- **Captured:** the retained source's identity, essential Box 2 label, bounded labels, and controlled evidence pointer were preserved without inference.
- **Incomplete:** one or more bounded fields were blank, obscured, cropped, unavailable, or not stated, while the retained source and essential Box 2 context could still be identified.
- **Unverified:** source identity, revision, copy designation, Box 2 label, legibility, or evidence control could not be preserved without inference.

Do not convert `captured` into `correct form`, `valid payer`, `verified recipient`, `payment reconciled`, `income reported`, `withholding correct`, `return complete`, or `tax resolved`.

Do not convert `incomplete` into `no account`, `no withholding`, `no state income`, `no payment`, or `no reporting duty`. A blank, missing, or obscured field is a source limitation, not proof of a real-world absence.

## Stop before retrieval, correction, or reconciliation

This workflow begins only after the form is already retained in an authorized system. Do not use it to:

- request, download, retrieve, or reconstruct an owner-specific form;
- call, email, mail, or otherwise contact a payer;
- match a payer, recipient, TIN, address, or account to another record;
- certify authenticity, completeness, correctness, or currentness;
- reconcile Box 2 to royalty statements, check details, deposits, division orders, production, title, or ownership;
- calculate gross income, withholding, basis, depletion, deductions, allocations, gain, loss, or tax;
- select, prepare, file, correct, or amend a return or information form;
- decide state sourcing, residence, filing, credit, or tax treatment; or
- connect the record to value, an offer, a sale, or a transaction decision.

If any of those tasks is needed, close this worksheet with its outcome and create a separately authorized record with the correct privacy, evidence, professional-review, and action controls.

## Keep the form and values controlled

The reusable public template does not need a screenshot or reproduction of the form. It also does not need a masked example TIN, sample account, fictional amount, or invented year. Those details can look real, leak into analytics, or be reused as if they were evidence.

Store the retained form and completed worksheet in an authorized controlled system. The public record may preserve only the blank field structure, source links, boundaries, and outcome definitions. If a reviewer needs to see the actual source, the retained-evidence pointer should route that authorized reviewer without copying the protected values into public content.

## Keep tax and transaction decisions separate

A Form 1099-MISC display is not a title record, ownership schedule, payment reconciliation, valuation input, offer test, or transaction authorization. It also does not replace current return instructions or a qualified tax professional's review of the owner's complete facts.

## Stop at authorized-human review

Close the worksheet with the selected outcome, exact limitation, unresolved question, and next authorized role. Do not answer the later tax or payment question inside the source-capture record.

Questions about form correction, payer contact, identity, authenticity, payment reconciliation, return preparation, federal or state treatment, basis, depletion, withholding, filing, amendment, title, ownership, valuation, an offer, or a transaction require the applicable payer, a qualified tax professional, legal counsel, a title professional, or another appropriately qualified reviewer.

This worksheet documents one source-capture event. It does not provide tax, legal, title, accounting, appraisal, valuation, investment, financial, identity-verification, offer, closing, or transaction advice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Box 2 prove which mineral property produced the payment?

No. This worksheet preserves the displayed Box 2 label and a controlled pointer only. Connecting a payment to a property, lease, well, tract, owner, or title record requires separate evidence and review.

### Can I compare the Box 2 value with my bank deposits here?

No. Deposit or statement reconciliation is a separate workflow with different source, period, aggregation, and exception controls. Record the controlled Box 2 pointer and stop.

### What if my retained copy uses a different revision?

Preserve that copy's exact displayed revision and calendar-year field. Do not replace it with the revision currently hosted on the IRS website, and do not declare the retained copy current or obsolete inside this worksheet.

### Should I record a blank state field as zero?

No. Record `blank`, `not shown`, or another exact source limitation. Zero is a value and must not be inferred from an empty field.

### Can this worksheet tell me whether to amend a return?

No. Amendment and filing decisions require the complete taxpayer file, current law and instructions, and qualified professional review. This record ends with source provenance and an authorized-human handoff.
