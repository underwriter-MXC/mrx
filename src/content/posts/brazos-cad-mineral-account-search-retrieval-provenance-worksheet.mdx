> **Destination-search boundary.** This blank worksheet documents one authorized manual attempt in the official Brazos Central Appraisal District Property Search. A displayed account does not establish identity, current ownership, title, mineral ownership, acreage, legal-description accuracy, appraisal correctness, tax liability, mineral market value, a property-to-well connection, offer quality, or whether to transact. Keep completed criteria, screenshots, results, and retained evidence in an authorized controlled system. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Brazos CAD mineral account search** can preserve where an appraisal-record lead came from without converting the lead into a conclusion. The worksheet records one official route, one source-displayed search mode, one criterion already supported by a controlled record, the displayed state, one retained-evidence pointer, the source's limitations, and one bounded outcome.

Use only `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`. These describe the attempt. They do not certify the account, person, property, mineral interest, appraisal, tax position, or transaction.

## Direct answer

Create one worksheet ID for one no-login manual attempt. Open the official [Brazos CAD Property Search](https://esearch.brazoscad.org/). Choose one source-displayed search mode, and select `Mineral` under Property Type when that option is available and fits the source-scoped task. Enter one exact criterion already present in a controlled record.

Record the access timestamp and timezone, official route, selected mode, exact criterion, source-displayed filters, displayed result or access state, source-displayed account-field labels, one controlled retained-evidence pointer, the current source limitations, and one bounded outcome. Stop before interpreting the result or combining it with another system.

Do not create an account, sign in, contact an office or contractor, submit a request, pay a fee, solve or bypass a CAPTCHA, automate queries, scrape, or bulk collect under this workflow.

## Keep the job inside one destination interface

The separate [Texas Comptroller county appraisal-district directory worksheet](/blog/texas-comptroller-county-appraisal-district-directory-retrieval-provenance-worksheet/) preserves the statewide route to a local office. This article begins after the official local destination has been identified. It records one attempt inside the current Brazos CAD search.

It is not a multi-source county locator. Do not add county deeds, tax-office accounts, Railroad Commission records, General Land Office records, operator data, production, royalty statements, private offers, or family documents as new search systems inside this worksheet. A retained source may supply the exact criterion, but its contents remain a separate evidence object.

Start a new worksheet ID or version when any of these changes:

- official destination route;
- access timestamp or timezone;
- selected search mode;
- Property Type selection;
- exact entered criterion;
- tax-year or another source-displayed filter;
- displayed result, error, or navigation state;
- retained-evidence pointer;
- source limitation; or
- controlled outcome.

One worksheet should make one attempt reproducible. It should not merge several queries until the researcher can no longer tell which criterion produced which state.

## Start with one criterion you already have

The current advanced search exposes fields such as owner name, abstract, Property ID, Property Type, Owner ID, Geographic ID, and Tax Year. The interface also exposes other property-oriented fields. Their presence does not authorize guessing.

Choose one mode supported by a record you are authorized to use:

- use an owner-name mode only when the controlled source supplies the spelling;
- use Property ID, Owner ID, or Geographic ID only when the controlled source supplies that exact identifier;
- use an abstract only when the controlled source displays the abstract reference; and
- use Tax Year only as a source-displayed filter, not as a conclusion about an account's history.

Preserve spacing, punctuation, suffixes, leading zeros, and capitalization when the interface accepts them. If the interface normalizes the entry, record both the entered criterion and the normalized display without silently replacing the source value.

Do not search a guessed relative, prior owner, operator, lease, address, abstract, account, or property identifier just to obtain a result. When the source does not supply an accepted criterion, use `unverified` and stop.

## Use the Mineral property type as a filter, not a conclusion

The current Brazos CAD interface includes `Mineral` in its Property Type list. Selecting it can narrow an appraisal-record attempt. It does not prove that a displayed account represents the private mineral interest described in another document.

A `Mineral` label does not establish:

- the identity of the current mineral owner;
- a chain of title or legal interest;
- net mineral acres, royalty acres, or a decimal interest;
- the accuracy or completeness of a legal description;
- a connection to a well, lease, operator, division order, or payor;
- a right to production or payment;
- the correctness of an appraisal; or
- a market value, offer range, or sale decision.

Preserve the source-displayed label and result state. Route identity, ownership, title, acreage, instrument, and legal-description questions to a qualified attorney, title professional, or land professional using complete evidence.

## Preserve the source's current warnings

The current search page states that displayed 2026 values are preliminary and may change during the appraisal review board season. It describes the information as being provided for research and says legal descriptions and acreage amounts are for appraisal-district use and should be verified before legal use or documents.

Preserve those warnings with the attempt. Do not rewrite `preliminary` as `incorrect`, `final`, or `fair`. Do not rewrite `research purposes` as certified evidence. Do not copy an acreage or legal-description field into a deed, title conclusion, offer, assessment, tax filing, or valuation model as an established fact.

The official [Brazos CAD Help and FAQs](https://esearch.brazoscad.org/Search/Help) says online records are not official and can change, describes current name, address, advanced-search, narrowing, and Property ID behavior, and states that mineral properties are not shown on its map because of missing map data. Preserve those source limits. Do not turn a missing map feature into proof that no mineral property or account exists. The help page also describes CSV download and email options, but this workflow does not download, email, or distribute results.

## Keep appraisal district and tax office roles separate

The [Brazos County Property Taxes page](https://www.brazoscountytx.gov/140/Property-Taxes) states that the tax assessor-collector provides consolidated tax assessment and collection services and routes specific property-tax account information to the county tax office. The [Texas Comptroller county directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/) separately routes users to local appraisal districts and tax assessor-collectors.

Do not merge those offices into one source. A Brazos CAD search result is not a tax-office receipt, payment record, tax certificate, bill, protest, exemption, or liability determination. This worksheet does not follow the result into the tax office and does not state what an owner owes.

[Texas Tax Code Chapter 25](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.25.htm) supplies statutory context for local appraisal records and appraisal rolls. It does not authorize this worksheet to decide whether a website entry is complete, correct, legally sufficient, owner-identifying, title-proving, tax-determinative, or relevant to a mineral sale.

## Use three outcomes without extending them

### Located

Use `located` when the current official interface displays a bounded result for the exact recorded mode, criterion, filters, and access event. Preserve the displayed state and source limitations.

Do not expand `located` into `matched owner`, `correct property`, `confirmed minerals`, `valid title`, `accurate acreage`, `completed valuation`, `tax verified`, or `sale-ready`.

### Not located

Use `not located` when the exact documented search completes and the interface displays no usable result for that attempt. Preserve the exact criterion, filters, route, time, and source state.

Do not expand `not located` into `no account`, `no property`, `no owner`, `no mineral interest`, `no appraisal`, `no tax record`, `no deed`, `no lease`, `no well`, or `no payment`. The record may use another name, identifier, year, classification, index, office, or access route.

### Unverified

Use `unverified` when the destination, mode, criterion, filter, access state, retained evidence, or source limitation prevents a bounded observation. Use it when the accepted criterion is unknown, the interface changes, results conflict, access fails, a login or contact is required, or several results cannot be distinguished without interpretation.

`Unverified` is not a negative finding. It prevents uncertainty from becoming a fact.

## Keep completed evidence controlled

The public template requires no completed search data. Store the actual criterion, screenshot, result, account fields, and retained source inside an authorized controlled system. Use a stable evidence pointer in the worksheet rather than copying sensitive details into a public link, email thread, or unrelated analysis service.

Preserve the source capture and a note of the access time. Do not crop away the interface state or limitation. If an authorized reviewer needs annotations, keep the unaltered capture and create a separate working copy. Do not overwrite the original with conclusions.

The result may be linked to a separate [retained evidence source-scope worksheet](/blog/mineral-rights-retained-evidence-source-scope-worksheet/). Keep the source artifact, this retrieval record, and any later professional opinion as separate evidence objects.

## Stop before appraisal, tax, title, or valuation work

This worksheet ends after the bounded outcome and limitation are recorded. Stop and route the question when the next action requires:

- contacting Brazos CAD, the tax office, an appraisal contractor, or another person;
- submitting a public-information request, form, protest, exemption, correction, or payment;
- creating an account, signing in, paying a charge, or bypassing an access control;
- determining whether a displayed account belongs to a person, estate, trust, entity, tract, or mineral interest;
- interpreting a legal description, deed, reservation, title chain, notice, appraisal method, or tax record;
- confirming acreage, ownership, royalty entitlement, a property-to-well connection, production, or payment;
- comparing an appraisal value with a market valuation, offer, or sale estimate;
- calculating basis, tax, gain, royalty, present value, or another financial amount; or
- deciding whether to accept an offer, choose a buyer, sell, retain, gift, exchange, or litigate.

Use the [Texas mineral tax value guide](/blog/why-doesnt-my-texas-mineral-tax-value-match-a-sale-estimate/) for general educational context about why a tax value and sale estimate can serve different purposes. It does not interpret this result. Official local-record questions belong with the applicable office; ownership, title, and legal-description questions belong with qualified legal or title professionals; tax questions belong with a CPA or qualified tax professional; and owner-specific value questions belong with an appropriately qualified valuation professional.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a located Brazos CAD mineral account prove mineral ownership?

No. Located means only that the official search displayed a bounded result for the exact recorded attempt. It does not establish identity, current ownership, title, net mineral acres, royalty entitlement, or legal effect.

### Does the displayed appraised value equal mineral market value?

No conclusion of that kind belongs in this worksheet. Brazos CAD currently labels displayed 2026 values preliminary, and an appraisal record serves a different purpose from an owner-specific market valuation or offer analysis.

### What if the exact search returns no result?

Record the route, timestamp, mode, criterion, filters, and displayed state, then use `not located` or `unverified`. An empty result is not proof that no account, property, mineral interest, tax record, deed, lease, well, or payment exists.

### Can the worksheet include a screenshot or real account number?

Keep completed screenshots, account identifiers, criteria, and results in an authorized controlled system. The reusable public worksheet and images stay blank and contain no real or fictional account data.

### Should I contact Brazos CAD from this workflow?

No. This workflow ends before contact, form submission, account creation, payment, or appraisal review. Use a separately authorized channel and the appropriate official or professional when a later question requires it.
