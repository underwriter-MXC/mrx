> **Evidence-boundary stop.** This blank worksheet records what one already-retained official Texas Secretary of State source excerpt displays. It does not search, retrieve, order, log in, file, submit, contact, compare parties, or take action. It does not establish identity, authority, legitimacy, legal status, service validity, ownership, mineral interests, offer fairness, transaction safety, or legal effect. Keep real values in an authorized controlled record. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

**Texas Secretary of State registered agent and registered office evidence boundary** is a transcription job. It creates a disciplined record of displayed fields and displayed limitations from one source excerpt that an authorized reviewer already has. It is not a business search, an identity cross-check, an order request, or a decision tool.

That distinction matters when a business-organization record appears near a mineral-rights offer. A registered-agent field and a registered-office field have specific source labels. They do not, by themselves, connect a person or address to a solicitation, signer, property, owner, mineral interest, offer, or transaction.

## Direct answer

Create one worksheet ID for one retained official source excerpt and one capture event. Preserve the source URL and title, capture time, exact entity name as displayed, exact registered-agent name as displayed, exact registered-office address as displayed, any source-displayed date or effective wording, a controlled evidence pointer, unresolved fields, and a source-limitation note.

Copy each permitted field exactly as displayed. If a field is absent, use `not displayed`. If it cannot be read, use `unreadable`. If the source or field meaning requires qualified review, use `unresolved` and name the next authorized reviewer.

Stop there. Do not search for another record, combine sources into a supposed match, compare the field with an offer sender, or decide what the display means for a person, entity, property, or transaction.

## Start with the official source boundary

The Texas Secretary of State's [Registered Agents](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/registeredagents.shtml) page says the Texas Business Organizations Code requires domestic or foreign filing entities to maintain a registered agent and office in Texas. The page describes a registered agent as the entity's agent on whom process, notice, or demand may be served.

The same page describes a registered office as a physical Texas address where the registered agent can be served with process during business hours. It also says the office is where the Secretary of State will mail correspondence and notes that the registered office may be the same as the entity's place of business.

Those are source descriptions, not conclusions about a retained record. The worksheet may preserve the exact displayed label and field value. It must not convert the source description into a statement that a particular person accepted service, represents an offer sender, controls an entity, owns minerals, or has authority to transact.

## Use one source excerpt per worksheet

Give the record a neutral identifier such as `[evidence-boundary worksheet ID]`. Associate it with one retained excerpt and one capture event. Do not combine several pages, screenshots, orders, search results, or correspondence items into a single displayed-field record.

Start a new worksheet version if any of these changes:

- the retained source excerpt;
- the official source URL or page title;
- the capture date or access context;
- the displayed entity name;
- the displayed registered-agent field;
- the displayed registered-office field;
- the source-displayed date or effective wording; or
- the evidence pointer or access control.

Version separation prevents a later reviewer from confusing two different captures or treating a changed display as one continuous record.

## Copy exactly or mark the gap

Transcribe the source label and value without shortening, expanding, normalizing, or correcting them. Preserve punctuation and qualifiers when they are displayed. If a line is clipped or ambiguous, mark it unreadable rather than guessing.

Do not silently replace the displayed entity name with a trade name from an offer. Do not replace the displayed registered-agent name with an officer, employee, attorney, service company, signer, caller, or person found elsewhere. Do not change a registered-office address to a mailing address, headquarters address, payment address, mineral-property address, or contact address.

The Texas Secretary of State's [Registered Agents FAQs](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/registeredagentfaqs.shtml) explains that an officer, owner, or employee may serve as registered agent, but an entity may not serve as its own registered agent. It also says an entity may contract with a service company for registered-agent services. That range is another reason not to infer a person's role from the field alone.

## Record source status without interpreting entity status

The worksheet may say whether the expected field was displayed, not displayed, unreadable, or unresolved in the retained excerpt. It may not say that the entity is active, in good standing, authorized, compliant, legitimate, trustworthy, safe, or suitable for a transaction.

It also must not infer that the registered agent consented, that an appointment is current, that a registered-office address is operational, or that a source excerpt satisfies a legal requirement. Those questions may depend on current records, dates, filings, law, or facts outside the excerpt.

When the display contains its own date or effective wording, copy that wording exactly. Do not turn an access date into an effective date. Do not turn a retained screenshot date into proof that a field remains current.

## Preserve a controlled evidence record

Real retained excerpts can contain personal or business information. Store the actual field values in an authorized system with access controls appropriate to the review. The public template should contain only blank labels.

The controlled record may preserve:

- worksheet ID and version;
- official URL and page title;
- capture timestamp and timezone;
- retained excerpt filename or record locator;
- integrity reference, when used;
- exact displayed fields;
- fields marked not displayed, unreadable, or unresolved;
- source limitations;
- authorized reviewer and access role; and
- retention and sharing rule.

Use the [document redaction checklist](/blog/mineral-rights-document-redaction-checklist-before-sharing-records/) before sharing an excerpt or completed worksheet through an approved channel. Do not place credentials, account data, private contact data, or transaction documents in the reusable public template.

## Stop at authorized-human review

Close the worksheet with one neutral state: `recorded for authorized review`, `held for source clarification`, or `not used`. Name the unresolved field and the next authorized reviewer. Do not use `identity confirmed`, `authority confirmed`, `safe to proceed`, or another decision statement.

An authorized human may decide that a separate search, record request, legal review, sender comparison, or transaction review is needed. That later work requires its own purpose, authority, sources, privacy controls, and evidence. It is outside this worksheet.
