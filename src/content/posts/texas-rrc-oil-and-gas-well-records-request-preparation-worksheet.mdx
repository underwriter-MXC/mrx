> **No-submit public-record boundary.** This article prepares one possible Texas Railroad Commission Oil and Gas Well Records request handoff. It does not submit a request, contact the agency, initiate a paid order, log in, scrape, automate, retrieve, or interpret a record. It does not determine title, ownership, acreage, property connection, lease effect, royalty decimal, payment entitlement, compliance, engineering or environmental status, production, reserves, value, offer quality, buyer suitability, legal effect, tax treatment, or transaction action. It does not provide legal, title, land, regulatory, engineering, environmental, geological, tax, accounting, appraisal, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC well records request preparation** worksheet is a no-submit handoff record. It preserves the official source route, access timestamp, requested record class, available locator fields, controlled private reference, requested scope, channel state, gaps, limitations, and responsible next owner before anyone contacts the agency or incurs a cost.

The output is preparation, not proof. It does not show that a record exists, that the Commission can locate it, that an online collection is complete, or that any record applies to a private mineral interest.

## Decide whether request preparation is the right job

The official [Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) page describes historical Central Records and Imaging materials and points readers to online systems. The separate [Well Records Online](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records-online/) page describes online record categories and stated coverage.

Use this worksheet only when the next authorized job is to prepare a scoped handoff for a possible well-record request. Do not use it to execute the Wellbore Query, search an Imaged Records profile, retrieve a well log, inspect a potential file, or document a completed request. Those are separate events with separate routes, evidence, and limits.

If an online or profile-specific workflow is already authorized, preserve that attempt in its own provenance worksheet. Do not silently convert its blocked, empty, or inconclusive state into a general records request.

## Start with current official source pages

Record the URL, access date, clock time, and timezone for each page actually used. The official [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory separates the Commission's research systems. It warns against automated volume retrieval and says its online data sets are continually updated, informational, non-authoritative, and without legal force.

That language controls the preparation record. A source page can identify a route or field without proving completeness, accuracy, currency, suitability, or private-property connection. Do not scrape, automate, repeat queries, or use workarounds to fill the worksheet.

The official [Well Records FAQs](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/about-us/faqs/oil-gas-faq/well-records-faqs/) provides record-type and search-troubleshooting context. Use it to frame a question or unresolved field, not to infer what a particular file contains.

## Record source-listed locator fields without manufacturing them

The Oil and Gas Well Records page asks requesters to provide as much useful locating information as possible. Its current list includes:

- the county where the property or well is located;
- an approximate date of drilling activity;
- the lease or well name and well number;
- the original or current operator name;
- the field name and reservoir;
- a Commission identifier, such as an API number, drilling-permit number, oil-lease number, or gas ID; and
- location details such as survey name, abstract, section, or block.

Preserve only a value supported by an authorized source. If a field has not been checked, mark **source not checked**. If the source was checked but the value is not established, mark **unknown**. If two sources conflict or the meaning is unclear, mark **unresolved** and assign a handoff. Do not guess, normalize, merge, or backfill a value merely to make the preparation packet look complete.

## Keep private locators in a controlled reference

Owner-specific counties, lease or well names, well numbers, operators, Commission identifiers, survey details, legal descriptions, and other locators can expose property or research context. The public worksheet should carry a neutral controlled-reference ID rather than unnecessary private detail.

The controlled reference should identify the authorized storage location, access level, and evidence owner. It should not copy a deed, lease, royalty statement, tax record, offer, or personal identifier into a public article or public-facing template.

## Separate record class from locator fields

The well-records page describes several types of materials that may appear in Commission files, including drilling-permit applications, completion reports, plugging reports, P-4 materials, plats, logs, correspondence, and miscellaneous filings. The online page separately describes potential records, oversized records, and identifier context.

Record the requested class in the request-preparation packet only when the source or authorized reviewer establishes it. Do not treat a broad phrase such as “well records” as permission to request every available record. Do not assume that one date range or online collection covers every well, district, file, format, or record class.

Keep these fields separate:

- **requested record class:** the narrow material being prepared for handoff;
- **requested scope or period:** the bounded range, if supported;
- **locator fields:** the values used to help identify a file;
- **channel state:** online route reviewed, form route reviewed, contact route identified, or not checked;
- **submission state:** always `not submitted` in this worksheet; and
- **cost state:** `not incurred` unless a separately authorized workflow records otherwise.

## Use neutral preparation states

Use only states that describe evidence already in the packet:

- **known:** a field is supported by an authorized source reference;
- **unknown:** the field is not established by the checked evidence;
- **source not checked:** no authorized source review has occurred for that field;
- **unresolved:** sources conflict, access failed, or the meaning needs qualified review; and
- **handoff required:** another authorized person or workflow must decide the next action.

Do not use **not found** or **does not exist** in a preparation-only record. Those phrases can imply that a search or request completed. This worksheet records neither.

## Preserve the channel without using it

The official well-records page identifies multiple request channels and points to a document price list. Preserve only the channel information needed for the handoff. Do not send an email, place a call, fax or mail a form, visit an office, submit through the internet, or start a paid research or copying request from this article workflow.

If a later authorized workflow may create a new or incremental charge, stop immediately before that charge, state the exact amount and purpose, and obtain the required payment approval. A page's reference to possible costs is not permission to incur them.

## Blank no-submit preparation template

| Field                     | What to preserve                                            |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Preparation ID            | Neutral identifier with no private locator                  |
| Official route            | Exact RRC page URL and access date                          |
| Prepared at               | Date, clock time, and explicit timezone                     |
| Requested record class    | Narrow source-supported class or unknown                    |
| Requested scope or period | Bounded range or unresolved                                 |
| County                    | Known, unknown, source not checked, or controlled reference |
| Drilling date             | Supported approximate date state or unknown                 |
| Lease or well locator     | Controlled-reference ID, not unnecessary public detail      |
| Operator                  | Supported state and controlled reference                    |
| Field or reservoir        | Supported state and controlled reference                    |
| Commission identifier     | Identifier type and controlled-reference ID                 |
| Survey or location        | Controlled-reference ID and evidence owner                  |
| Channel state             | Reviewed, not checked, or handoff required                  |
| Submission state          | Not submitted                                               |
| Cost state                | Not incurred                                                |
| Gaps and conflicts        | Unknown, unresolved, access limitation, or source conflict  |
| Limitations               | Prohibited inference and route boundary                     |
| Handoff owner             | Authorized next reviewer and narrow next task               |

Keep the template blank until actual authorized evidence supplies a field. Never insert example wells, operators, identifiers, properties, records, prices, or findings that could be mistaken for real evidence.

## Handoff without expanding the claim

A useful handoff lets the next authorized reviewer see which official route was consulted, when it was accessed, which record class is requested, which locator fields are supported, where controlled references are stored, which fields remain unknown, and what action is still prohibited.

The handoff must not say that the Commission has the requested record, that the record is complete, or that it belongs to the owner's property. It must not bridge a regulatory-record locator to a deed, lease, royalty interest, payment right, production history, valuation model, offer, or transaction without separate evidence and qualified review.

The preparation job is complete when one neutral packet preserves the route, fields, scope, gaps, limits, and responsible next owner. Submission, payment, retrieval, interpretation, and decision-making remain separate workflows.
