> **Blank-form inventory boundary.** This page inventories only the current blank Texas Comptroller Form 50-843 reached from the official Crane CAD Forms page. It records source-control facts and printed blank field labels. It does not select a tax official, collect owner data, decide whether electronic delivery is appropriate, complete or sign the request, contact an office, submit anything, calculate timing, interpret legal effect, or prove delivery or acceptance. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Form 50-843 Crane mineral property** inventory should answer a narrow question: what does the current blank official source display before anyone enters owner-specific information? Record the exact source URL, displayed title and form number, revision line, page count, six numbered section headings, blank field labels, retrieval date, time and time zone, controlled-copy reference, source limitation, and one document-control state.

Use only `recorded`, `not displayed`, `revision conflict`, or `authorized-human-stop`. These labels describe source-control work. They do not mean complete, correct, selected, authorized, signed, filed, accepted, delivered, timely, legally effective, or appropriate for a particular owner.

## Confirm the source before recording fields

Begin at the current [Crane CAD Forms page](https://www.cranecad.org/Forms). On the access date shown above, that official page linked **Request for Electronic Delivery of Communications with a Tax Official**, Form 50-843. Preserve both the forms-page URL and the exact linked-PDF URL so a later reviewer can see how the blank source was obtained.

Create one neutral inventory header with:

- **inventory ID:** a nonidentifying internal reference;
- **source page:** the official Crane CAD Forms URL;
- **linked source:** the exact Form 50-843 PDF URL;
- **retrieved at:** date, time and time zone;
- **displayed title and form number:** copied from the blank source;
- **displayed revision line:** copied exactly, without guessing what it means;
- **page count as retrieved:** the pages present in that source copy;
- **controlled-copy reference:** a pointer to the retained authorized copy;
- **source limitation:** blank-source inventory only; and
- **state:** one of the four allowed labels.

Do not begin with an email attachment, completed request, owner file, search snippet, third-party upload, or prior-year copy. If the official link is unavailable or the retrieved source identity cannot be confirmed, use `authorized-human-stop`.

## Keep each section inside its literal boundary

### Section 1: selection labels only

The blank form presents categories for a tax-official selection. The inventory may record the exact labels displayed, but it must not check a box, identify the correct recipient, choose a taxing unit, resolve an `other` category, or recommend a destination.

The [Texas Comptroller Crane County directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/crane.php) separates local property-tax offices and links the official appraisal-district site. That directory supports source identity, not an owner-specific routing decision. If a destination must be chosen, end this inventory and route the question to an authorized human using current official instructions.

### Sections 2 and 3: private-value stop

The Property Owner and Authorized Representative sections contain blank identity, contact, address, authority, and role fields. Inventory the printed labels only. Do not enter a real or fictional person, business, phone number, address, role, authority basis, or representative relationship in a public example.

An apparently obvious authority category is still an owner-specific legal fact. The inventory does not decide who may act, whether an appointment is effective, or which evidence would establish authority.

### Section 4: property labels without property facts

The current blank source displays account-number and legal-description labels. Record those labels as part of the form structure, but do not retrieve an account or transfer a value from the [Crane CAD property-search surface](https://www.cranecad.org/home). The search surface supplies local context and includes a mineral and industrial property type; this workflow performs no search and makes no ownership, acreage, tax, appraisal, or value inference.

### Section 5: email label without an address

Record the displayed email-address label only. Do not collect, validate, normalize, publish, test, or send to an address. The inventory does not choose a communication channel or establish that any address is controlled by an owner or representative.

### Section 6: certification labels without execution

Record the printed certification-and-signature heading and blank signature-related labels as source structure. Do not copy the certification into an owner-specific instruction, sign, date, witness, notarize, authenticate, or submit anything. The inventory does not determine whether a statement is true, whether authority exists, or what legal consequence may follow.

## Preserve filing text as source observation, not instruction

The current blank PDF includes general information, filing instructions, definitions, a duty-to-notify heading, electronic-procedure context, and a no-fee statement. The [Texas Comptroller forms index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/forms/) supplies current statewide form-source context, while Texas Tax Code [Chapter 1](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docs/tx/pdf/tx.1.pdf) supplies governing-source provenance.

An inventory may note that those sections are present and preserve their page locations. It must not tell an owner whether to elect electronic delivery, which official applies, what supporting documentation is needed, how to complete a field, when a notice is due, whether a change was effective, what a communication method means, or whether delivery occurred. Those questions require current official material, owner-specific facts, and qualified review.

## Use the four document-control states literally

### Recorded

`Recorded` means the exact displayed source-control fact or blank field label was copied with a source locator. It says nothing about a completed request or owner-specific result.

### Not displayed

`Not displayed` means the defined label was not found in the specific retrieved source during the recorded review. It does not prove that the label never existed, should appear, or has legal significance.

### Revision conflict

`Revision conflict` means two retained blank sources display different form numbers, revision lines, section headings, or field labels. Preserve both source URLs, retrieval times, hashes or controlled references, and exact observed text. Do not merge the sources, carry a value from one into the other, or decide which version controls.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before office selection, private-data entry, identity or authority determination, form completion, signature, contact, upload, email, filing, submission, payment, deadline calculation, delivery conclusion, legal interpretation, tax guidance from a qualified professional, appraisal judgment, valuation, representation, or strategy.

The workflow ends at the stop. It does not draft a message, open a portal, enter credentials, choose a checkbox, attach documentation, or send a request.

## Separate source control from contact and delivery evidence

This field inventory proves only what one blank official source displayed at one retrieval time. It does not prove that anyone completed or submitted a request, that an office received or accepted it, that a communication was sent, or that delivery was confirmed.

Keep adjacent evidence objects separate:

- **blank source inventory:** the current official form structure;
- **owner-controlled working copy:** any private values entered by an authorized person;
- **submission evidence:** any record created by an authorized submission process;
- **communication record:** any later item sent or received; and
- **delivery evidence:** only what an identified source actually states about delivery.

Do not backfill the blank-source inventory with facts from those later objects. If contact routing becomes necessary, the separate [contact-routing worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-industrial-account-contact-routing-worksheet/) illustrates the boundary: record a current source-displayed destination without performing outreach. It does not choose a destination for Crane County or authorize contact.

## Prepare a controlled review handoff

A neutral handoff packet may contain the inventory ID, official source URLs, retrieval timestamp, displayed form identity and revision line, page count, section and field-label index, controlled-copy reference, exact conflict note, source limitations, one document-control state, and one unresolved question.

A safe question is: `Which current official source or qualified review is required before an authorized person makes the owner-specific decision?`

Before any disclosure or action, an authorized human should verify the recipient, role, purpose, minimum necessary data, approved channel, and current official requirements. Keep the reusable public inventory blank.

- no form was completed, signed, contacted, uploaded, emailed, filed, or submitted;

- owner-specific interpretation or action ends at `authorized-human-stop`.

Forms, links, field labels, and official procedures can change. Re-check the current official source whenever the inventory is used.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does recorded mean the form is ready to file?

No. It means only that a source-control fact or blank field label was copied from the identified source. It does not establish completeness, correctness, authority, signature, filing, acceptance, delivery, timeliness, or legal effect.

### Can the inventory choose the tax official?

No. It may record blank selection labels but never chooses an office, recipient, taxing unit, or category.

### Can the public template contain owner information?

No. Keep all owner, representative, property, contact, signature, and completed-form data in an authorized controlled system. The reusable public inventory stays blank.

### What if two saved sources show different revision lines?

Preserve both exact sources and mark `revision conflict`. Do not merge them, transfer values, or decide which controls.

### Does the inventory prove electronic delivery?

No. It proves no election, submission, acceptance, communication, or delivery event. It also does not interpret timing, rescission, consent, or legal effect.
