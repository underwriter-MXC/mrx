> **Public retrieval boundary.** This article records one authorized manual attempt through the Texas Railroad Commission's Imaged Records **District Office Well Records** profile. It does not interpret a document or determine completeness, well identity or status, permits, completions, production, reserves, safety, environmental condition, compliance, property connection, ownership, title, lease, royalty, payment, acreage, value, offer quality, legal effect, tax, or transaction outcome. It does not provide legal, title, land, regulatory, environmental, geological, engineering, tax, accounting, appraisal, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC District Office Well Records retrieval** worksheet preserves how one manual source attempt occurred. It identifies the official route and profile, timestamps the attempt, records source-displayed historical scope, points to controlled criteria, keeps access and result states separate, retains evidence, and assigns one bounded outcome.

Use only **located**, **not located**, or **unverified**. These labels describe one retrieval event. They do not transform an archive profile, source label, document link, image, or empty state into a conclusion about a well, property, owner, lease, regulator action, valuation, offer, or transaction.

## Start with the official query directory

Open the Texas RRC [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory and retain its current Oil and Gas Imaged Records entry route. Before opening any profile, record the directory URL, displayed source label, access date, clock time, timezone, and menu destination.

The separate [Imaged Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/imaged-records/) page supplies the official path into the imaged-record family. Preserve that route as provenance. Do not infer that a record exists merely because the family is available, and do not switch to a neighboring profile or electronic query because it appears easier to use.

This worksheet covers one authorized manual attempt. It does not create an automated research process. Do not scrape, poll, retrieve in volume, bypass access controls, automate CAPTCHA handling, or work around validation, timeout, session, or viewer behavior. When the official path cannot be completed as authorized, record the stop and use **unverified**.

## Lock profileId 27 and its narrow scope

The official [Imaged Records Query Menu](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/imaged-records/imaged-records-menu/) identifies **District Office Well Records** and links it as `profileId=27`. Preserve the menu URL, displayed profile label, link destination, and access timestamp. The profile ID is route provenance; it is not a well identifier and proves nothing about a specific record.

The menu describes this collection as district-office well records that predate 1980 and did not have a lease number or API number when the records were created. Record that statement as **source-described collection scope** with the access date. It does not establish the age, identity, completeness, availability, or relevance of any particular file.

| Route or collection                                | Boundary for this worksheet                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| District Office Well Records profileId 27          | Preserve one manual attempt, displayed states, evidence, and source-described scope.    |
| Potential, Dry Hole, and Well Log profiles         | Separate imaged-record collections; do not merge their links, images, or result states. |
| Wellbore, Drilling Permit, and Completions queries | Separate electronic systems with different criteria, coverage, and response states.     |
| P-4, P-17, production, GIS, EDMS, and hearings     | Separate sources requiring separate attempt records and boundaries.                     |
| Other district-office profiles or Energy Depot     | Separate routes; never substitute them under the profileId 27 attempt.                  |
| Paid records research                              | Outside this public, no-new-charge worksheet.                                           |

The collection name is not a substantive finding. It does not show that a well lacked an identifier later, that a responsive image is complete, that a file belongs to a reader's property, or that a private lease or royalty interest exists.

## Preserve the current access result without embellishment

During article preparation, a direct request to the official profile route at `profileId=27` returned HTTP `403`. No profile page was captured, no criteria controls were verified, no values were entered, and no query completed. The defensible outcome for that attempt is **unverified**.

An access response is not a search result. HTTP `403`, a blocked page, an error, a timeout, a validation failure, a default screen, an interrupted session, a CAPTCHA, an unavailable viewer, or any incomplete attempt does not show that a record is absent. Preserve the route, timestamp, response state, stop point, and evidence reference without attempting a bypass.

## Build the attempt header before using any controls

Assign a neutral attempt ID that does not expose a property, well, party, ownership, or transaction value. Then preserve:

- the official directory, Imaged Records, menu, and profile URLs;
- the displayed District Office Well Records label and `profileId=27`;
- the access date, clock time, and explicit timezone;
- the source-described historical coverage note and its access date;
- the authorized manual access method;
- the exact source-displayed criteria labels, if the profile loads;
- a controlled criteria-set reference and exact entered or selected values in that controlled record;
- the manual submit control or method;
- the displayed access, validation, submission, result, or interruption state;
- any document-link, image, download, or viewer state actually presented;
- the retained evidence location, access restrictions, and file integrity reference; and
- the transfer destination, recipient limits, unresolved questions, and recheck requirement.

Do not publish well names, locations, districts, fields, operators, dates, or private party values merely because a source accepts or displays them. Keep necessary values in an authorized controlled location and let the worksheet point to that record.

## Keep digitization context separate from attempt evidence

The RRC's [October 2024 digitization announcement](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/news/102924rrc-s-digitally-imaged-oil-and-gas-records-top-83-million/) explains that district-office paper and microfilm records were digitized and directs researchers to the Imaged Records menu. That context supports the official route and general archive history. It does not prove that a particular record is online, searchable, complete, or related to a specific mineral interest.

Record three layers separately:

1. **Source context:** what the official page says about the collection and digitization effort.
2. **Attempt state:** what the authorized manual session actually displayed or prevented.
3. **Document state:** whether a responsive link, image, download, or viewer state was presented.

Never fill a gap in one layer with language from another. A broad digitization count cannot establish profile completeness. A profile label cannot establish a well fact. A viewer opening cannot establish a property relationship.

## Separate public-record mechanics from private-rights questions

The official [Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) page supplies historical record-format and request context. It also explains that the RRC does not have authority over some private-rights matters. Use that page to define source and agency boundaries, not to answer ownership, title, lease, royalty, or payment questions.

If a responsive item appears later, preserve only what the source displayed: profile, criteria reference, result row or link state, viewer state, retrieval timestamp, and evidence reference. A separate qualified review would be needed for document interpretation or any substantive property, regulatory, engineering, environmental, valuation, legal, tax, or transaction question.

## Apply fail-closed outcome rules

Use **located** only when a completed, attributable manual attempt in profileId 27 displays responsive material. Located means that the recorded source attempt displayed something; it does not mean complete, correct, current, relevant, connected to a property, or legally sufficient.

Use **not located** only when every condition below is true:

1. the official District Office Well Records profile loaded;
2. the displayed criteria labels were recorded;
3. the controlled criteria were accepted;
4. the manual submission completed;
5. the source displayed an empty result attributable to those criteria; and
6. no block, error, timeout, validation, session, CAPTCHA, viewer, or interpretation issue makes the attempt inconclusive.

Use **unverified** for every other state. The current HTTP `403` attempt is unverified. Do not repeat queries, weaken criteria, change systems, purchase research, or use a neighboring profile merely to produce a different outcome label.

## Final quality check

Before closing the record, confirm that:

- the attempt began at the official Imaged Records route and preserved profileId 27;
- the timestamp includes a timezone;
- the pre-1980/no-lease-or-API description remains a dated source-scope note, not a substantive conclusion;
- source labels and any displayed criteria labels are exact;
- actual criteria values remain controlled and are not unnecessarily published;
- HTTP `403`, blocked, error, timeout, validation, default, session, CAPTCHA, and viewer states remain **unverified**;
- **not located** appears only after a completed empty result attributable to accepted recorded criteria;
- document, viewer, evidence, scope, and transfer states remain separate;
- adjacent imaged-record, electronic query, district-office, Energy Depot, and paid research routes were not merged;
- no automation, bulk retrieval, repeated-query workflow, bypass, CAPTCHA handling, or workaround occurred; and
- no document interpretation or well, property, ownership, lease, production, environmental, compliance, value, offer, legal, tax, or transaction conclusion was added.

If any item fails, retain **unverified**. The worksheet succeeds when it accurately preserves what happened, including a blocked attempt, without turning archive mechanics into a substantive conclusion.
