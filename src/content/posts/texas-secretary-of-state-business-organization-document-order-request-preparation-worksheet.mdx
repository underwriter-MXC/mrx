> **Preparation-only stop boundary.** This worksheet organizes information for one possible Texas Secretary of State business-organization document order request. It does not place an order, reserve a service, create an agency record, authorize a charge, log in, run a search, verify a certificate, submit by any route, purchase a document, or collect payment data. Keep the reusable worksheet blank. An authorized human must independently review it before any separately approved submission or purchase. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

**Texas Secretary of State business organization document order request preparation** is a record-building job, not an agency transaction. The worksheet preserves what an authorized reviewer may need to decide whether a separate request should be prepared further. It stops before the first action that could access an account, send data, create a record, retrieve a document, or incur a fee.

That stop matters when public-record research sits near an offer review. A blank request-preparation record can make the source, intended output, unresolved fields, and next owner visible. It cannot authenticate a contact person, establish representative authority, prove property ownership, interpret a document, or answer whether an offer is legitimate or fair.

## Direct answer

Create one neutral request-preparation ID. Link it to the current official agency page, preserve the access date, identify one intended agency-listed route, and keep separate blank fields for the entity reference, requested output, copy type, quantity, delivery choice, requester-controlled inputs, evidence pointers, and unresolved items.

Use exact agency terminology. Keep a `certificate of fact - status` separate from a Comptroller `certificate of account status`. If the source, entity, output, copy type, quantity, delivery choice, or route changes, create a new worksheet version or request-preparation ID.

End at authorized-human review. Do not log in, search, enter payment information, calculate a total, submit, email, mail, purchase, or verify a certificate through this worksheet.

## Start from the current official source

The Texas Secretary of State's [Copies and Certificates](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/copies.shtml) page currently lists three ways that copies or certificates may be ordered: online through SOSDirect, by email, and by mail. Record only the route label an authorized reviewer is considering. Do not turn that descriptive choice into operational instructions.

For each agency statement used in the preparation record, preserve:

- the official URL;
- the official page title;
- the access date and timezone;
- the exact relevant agency wording or field label;
- a controlled evidence pointer; and
- whether the item is still current, changed, unavailable, or unresolved at review time.

Search-result snippets and copied summaries are not controlling sources. Return to the official page before relying on a route, product name, field, or published fee.

## Define one request before adding fields

Give the worksheet a neutral identifier such as `[request preparation ID]`. Then state one narrow intended output using the source's terminology. The preparation record should not combine multiple entities or treat several document types as one request.

Start a separate worksheet version or request-preparation ID if any of these changes:

- requested entity;
- requested document, filing, or certificate type;
- plain or certified copy choice;
- quantity;
- delivery or return choice;
- agency route being considered; or
- source page whose current instructions control the preparation.

Version separation prevents a later reviewer from mistaking one unresolved choice for a completed request. It also creates a clean stopping point when the source changes.

## Label each field by provenance

Do not fill a blank because another source appears to contain a likely answer. Assign every field one of four provenance labels:

1. **Agency-required or agency-described.** The current official source names or describes the field, route, product, or choice.
2. **Requester-controlled input.** An authorized requester would supply the value for the defined purpose.
3. **Controlled evidence pointer.** The public worksheet stores only a locator to evidence held in an authorized system.
4. **Unresolved.** The value, need, source, or reviewer remains open and must not be inferred.

Keep the real values outside the reusable public template. That includes entity names, SOS file numbers, document references, filing dates or numbers, requester names, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, account details, payment data, screenshots, and retained correspondence.

- **Official page and access time:** Controlled evidence pointer; `[source record pointer]`
- **Agency route considered:** Agency-required or agency-described; `[SOSDirect, email, or mail route label]`
- **Entity name:** Requester-controlled input; `[controlled entity name]`
- **SOS file number, if available:** Requester-controlled input; `[controlled SOS file number]`
- **Requested document, filing, or certificate:** Agency-required or agency-described; `[exact source term]`
- **Plain, certified, or certificate choice:** Agency-required or agency-described; `[exact source choice]`
- **Known document description or filing reference:** Requester-controlled input; `[controlled reference]`
- **Quantity:** Requester-controlled input; `[quantity]`
- **Delivery or return choice:** Agency-required or agency-described; `[source-described choice]`
- **Dated published-fee source note:** Controlled evidence pointer; `[fee-source pointer and access date]`
- **Unresolved fields:** Unresolved; `[open item and next reviewer]`
- **Authorized-human review:** Requester-controlled input; `[reviewer and review date]`

The bracketed text is a field label, not an example value. Do not replace it with fictional data for a public illustration. A fictional entity or file number can look real after the worksheet is copied away from its explanation.

## Preserve exact copy and certificate terms

The agency page names certified copies and `certificates of fact - status`. Its [SOSDirect ordering instructions](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/instructions-for-copies.shtml) separately describe certificates, certified copies, and plain copies. Preserve the exact requested output and copy choice rather than collapsing them into a generic label such as “official record.”

The Secretary of State also explains that its certificate of fact - status is often confused with a Comptroller certificate of account status. The two come from different agencies and describe different records. Do not rename either one as a generic “certificate of good standing” in the request-preparation row.

Exact naming is still not interpretation. This worksheet does not decide which record is legally sufficient for a purpose. If sufficiency or legal effect matters, an authorized reviewer should take the actual requirement and the current agency descriptions to qualified counsel or the requesting institution.

## Record fee context without authorizing a charge

The official instructions page accessed on August 26, 2026, currently lists a $15 charge per certificate of fact - status. It describes certified copies as a $15 certificate charge plus $1 per page. It lists plain copies at $0.10 per page and describes a separate $1 online search fee that may be waived in the circumstance stated on the page.

These figures are dated source facts, not a quote for a planned request. Do not calculate a total, predict page counts, state that payment is due, or copy the amounts into a payment authorization. Record only the official page, access date, exact fee statement or controlled excerpt pointer, and an unresolved instruction to confirm current requirements with the agency before any separately authorized transaction.

Never place a card number, bank detail, client account, billing address, login, temporary-session code, or payment-method choice in this worksheet.

## Keep descriptive routes non-operational

The agency currently lists SOSDirect, email, and mail. A preparation worksheet may preserve which label an authorized reviewer is considering and why a required field is unresolved. It must not provide or execute the operational sequence for any route.

Do not:

- create or access an SOSDirect account or temporary session;
- run an entity or filing-number search;
- enter requester or payment information;
- add an order item;
- prepare an email for sending;
- address or dispatch a mailing;
- submit an order;
- retrieve a document; or
- use certificate verification.

Those are separate actions with separate access, privacy, authority, and possible charge consequences. The worksheet ends before them.

## Separate request preparation from sender review

The [sender identity cross-check](/blog/mineral-rights-offer-sender-identity-cross-check/) organizes stated sender details across independent sources. This worksheet does none of that comparison. It must not connect a requested or returned SOS record to a person, signer, email address, phone number, website, solicitation, offer, property, or proposed transaction.

The [Texas Comptroller search worksheet](/blog/texas-comptroller-taxable-entity-search-retrieval-provenance-worksheet/) preserves one manual account-status search attempt. This worksheet does not perform a search. It only organizes a possible document request for later review.

A requested record may become one controlled source for a separate, qualified review. The preparation row itself cannot establish identity, affiliation, authority, legitimacy, authenticity, endorsement, capacity, reputation, fairness, fraud, standing beyond exact agency wording, financial ability, title, ownership, property connection, value, legal effect, document sufficiency, offer quality, or transaction quality.

## Retain a controlled source record

For each agency statement, keep a source record in an authorized system with:

- request-preparation ID and worksheet version;
- official URL and final destination;
- official page title;
- access timestamp and timezone;
- exact field label or relevant agency wording;
- capture or note filename;
- SHA-256 or another integrity reference, when used;
- reviewer, access role, and retention rule; and
- whether another reviewer reproduced the source observation.

The public worksheet receives only the evidence pointer. Use the [document redaction checklist](/blog/mineral-rights-document-redaction-checklist-before-sharing-records/) before sharing any later request or returned record through an approved channel.

## Stop at authorized-human review

Close the worksheet with one status: `prepared for authorized review`, `held for source clarification`, or `not pursued`. Name the unresolved field and the next authorized reviewer. Do not use `ready to submit` as an automatic result.

Completing the worksheet does not establish that the requested record exists, is available, is sufficient, has legal effect, concerns a particular sender or property, or supports any offer or transaction conclusion. The authorized human must independently confirm the current source, request purpose, fields, privacy controls, authority, fee context, and next action.

Only after that separate review may someone with the necessary authority decide whether to begin a submission or purchase. That later action is outside this worksheet.
