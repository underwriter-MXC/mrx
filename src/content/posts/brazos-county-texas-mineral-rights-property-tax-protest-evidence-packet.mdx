> **Evidence-assembly boundary.** This blank worksheet begins only after a Brazos mineral appraisal account or retained notice is already identified. It organizes sources; it does not search for an account, route contact, file a protest, complete or sign a form, upload a document, disclose private data, pay a fee, interpret a deadline or statute, select an argument or representative, determine ownership or exemption, judge evidentiary sufficiency, calculate taxable or market value, or predict a result. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Brazos County mineral rights property tax protest** evidence packet should be a reproducible index, not a persuasive conclusion. Start with a controlled reference to the already-identified account or notice. Then create seven sections: packet cover, source ledger, document inventory, issue matrix, discrepancy log, privacy boundary, and submission-readiness stop.

Use only four workflow states: `indexed`, `not located`, `conflict recorded`, and `authorized-human-stop`. Those states describe document handling. They do not mean verified, accepted, sufficient, correct, complete, submitted, persuasive, or successful.

## Build the packet cover first

The cover tells a later reviewer exactly which record set they are looking at without exposing private data in the reusable template.

- **Packet ID:** Use a neutral controlled reference, not an owner name, address, account number, owner key, property ID, or legal description.
- **Starting record:** Preserve the exact title of the retained notice or official source page. Do not rewrite the source name into a conclusion.
- **Tax year:** Copy the year exactly as displayed. Do not infer it from a download date or a different record.
- **Access record:** Record the source URL, access date, time, and time zone.
- **Owner-selected issue label:** Use a neutral label such as `displayed field requires review`. Do not write `appraisal is wrong` or another assumed conclusion.
- **Packet state:** Mark each retained item `draft`, `current`, or `archived`, together with the basis for that label.
- **Privacy class:** Mark the packet controlled when it contains any owner, account, notice, property, legal-description, acreage, value, tax, production, lease, offer, or correspondence data.

The public example remains blank. The completed cover belongs in an authorized controlled system.

## Create a source ledger that another reviewer can reproduce

Give every source a separate row. Record the issuer, exact page or document title, HTTPS URL, revision or effective date when the source displays one, access time, the statement or field the source supports, and the source limitation.

The current [Brazos CAD Property Search](https://esearch.brazoscad.org/) provides the local account-source context and displays limitations on research use and legal-description or acreage reliance. This workflow does not search that interface. If account retrieval is still required, use the separate [Brazos CAD mineral account search and retrieval-provenance worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-account-search-retrieval-provenance-worksheet/) and stop there before building this packet.

The [mineral appraisal interface](https://mineral.cagi.com/Main?modulenum=242) also creates a firm access boundary. A login or owner-specific record is not needed to finish a blank packet template. Record `authorized-human-stop` rather than creating credentials, testing access, or exposing a notice or identifier.

Do not merge multiple sources into a single `official records` row. Separate provenance is what allows a reviewer to see whether two documents cover the same issue, the same period, and the same publisher.

## Inventory the records without deciding what they prove

Use four document categories. A category is an organizational label, not a statement that a record is admissible, complete, accurate, or controlling.

1. **Official notice or account record.** Index the retained notice and the already-identified official account page, if available. Preserve the exact title, publisher, tax year, retrieval time, and limitation.
2. **Owner-controlled records.** Index correspondence, retained statements, prior notices, and internal notes only when the owner is authorized to retain them. Keep the underlying files private.
3. **Official identity or production records, if actually used.** Record the exact agency source, covered period, retrieval time, and limitation. Do not use a production record to prove ownership, title, acreage, or an appraisal conclusion.
4. **Receipts and correspondence.** Preserve confirmation identifiers, dates, sender or issuer, subject, and controlled location. A receipt proves only what it states; it does not prove acceptance, sufficiency, or outcome.

For every row, add `supports which statement?` and `does not establish`. The second field prevents a source from silently expanding beyond its actual role.

## Build an issue matrix by field and period

An issue matrix helps a reviewer locate relevant records without turning packet assembly into appraisal or legal analysis.

Create one row per source-displayed field or owner-selected question. Record:

- the neutral issue label;
- the exact source and document title;
- the field as displayed, without rewriting it;
- the period or tax year shown;
- the access or document date;
- the controlled file reference;
- the source limitation; and
- one status: `indexed`, `not located`, `conflict recorded`, or `authorized-human-stop`.

Do not add a `correct value`, `proper owner`, `winning argument`, or `recommended adjustment` column. Those would convert administrative indexing into professional judgment.

## Preserve discrepancies instead of resolving them

When two records do not align, open a discrepancy row. Record the exact field, source A, source B, each source's relevant period, and whether the difference is `not located` or `conflict recorded`.

Write neutrally:

- `The retained notice and the current account display use different text for this field.`
- `The covered periods are not the same.`
- `A source for this statement was not located in the controlled packet.`
- `The source limitation prevents a conclusion from this worksheet.`

Do not write:

- `The district made an error.`
- `This record proves the owner is correct.`
- `The lower figure should control.`
- `The minerals are worth this amount.`
- `This evidence will win the protest.`

A discrepancy log preserves the question for an authorized reviewer. It does not choose which source controls, interpret the law, or decide whether the difference is material.

## Keep appraisal administration separate from mineral-sale value

The [Texas Comptroller protest resource](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/protests/) provides statewide context for the property-tax protest and evidence process. The [property-tax forms index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/forms/) provides a current official location for statewide forms. Preserve the exact current source and form identity if one is used; do not select, complete, sign, or submit a form from this worksheet.

The [Appraisal Review Board resource](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/arb/) describes the ARB's role in the local property-tax system. Packet assembly does not speak for an ARB and cannot determine whether a document will be accepted, considered sufficient, or given any weight.

Texas Tax Code [Chapter 41](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docs/tx/pdf/tx.41.pdf) supplies the statutory process context. This page does not interpret that chapter for an owner, calculate a deadline, identify a protest ground, or recommend a legal position.

An appraisal-system figure is also not automatically a mineral-sale estimate. Do not compare it with an offer or convert it into a market-value conclusion here. Use the separate explanation of [why a Texas mineral tax value may not match a sale estimate](/blog/why-doesnt-my-texas-mineral-tax-value-match-a-sale-estimate/) when that distinction is the actual question.

## Apply a strict private-data boundary

The public template and images should contain no real owner name, mailing address, email, phone number, account number, owner key, property ID, geographic ID, notice image, legal description, acreage, decimal interest, value, tax amount, lease, well, operator, payor, production record, offer, signature, or correspondence.

Store completed packet files in an authorized controlled system. Use neutral pointers such as `CONTROLLED-NOTICE-REF` in the index. Before any disclosure, an authorized human should verify the recipient, purpose, approved channel, minimum necessary fields, retention requirement, and current instructions.

Do not upload every item merely because it is indexed. Inventory is not submission advice. Privacy, relevance, format, disclosure, and procedural decisions belong to an authorized human using the applicable current official materials or qualified professional.

## Stop at submission readiness

Before anyone acts on the packet, run this administrative readiness check:

- the account or notice was already identified before this workflow began;
- each source has an exact title, issuer, URL, access time, and limitation;
- the displayed tax year or covered period is preserved without inference;
- every document has a controlled reference and current state;
- each issue row points to the exact source field it indexes;
- missing records and conflicting fields remain neutral;
- private data remains outside the public template;
- no value, ownership, exemption, tax-liability, deadline, legal-right, sufficiency, strategy, representative, or outcome conclusion appears; and
- the next step is `authorized-human-stop` before contact, form completion, signature, login, upload, submission, payment, or disclosure.

The [Texas Comptroller local-property directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/) distinguishes appraisal-district functions from tax assessor-collector functions. That boundary can prevent a packet from mixing appraisal evidence with tax-payment records, but it does not decide an owner-specific destination. If a current route must be recorded, use the separate [Brazos CAD contact-routing worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-industrial-account-contact-routing-worksheet/) and keep outreach outside that worksheet too.

Current notices and official procedures can change. Verify the instructions, deadlines, channels, file requirements, and confirmation method displayed by the applicable official source before any action. If a source is unavailable, inconsistent, or requires private access, mark `authorized-human-stop`; do not guess from a search snippet, old form, prior-year notice, or another owner's packet.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this packet prove that I own the mineral interest?

No. It organizes source-labeled records for review. It is not a title search, ownership opinion, legal-description verification, or determination that any record controls.

### Can this worksheet tell me whether the taxable value is correct?

No. It does not calculate, compare, or validate market value, taxable value, mineral-sale value, exemptions, tax liability, or appraisal methods. Those questions require the applicable official process and qualified review.

### Which protest deadline should I use?

This worksheet does not calculate a deadline. Preserve the date and instructions displayed on the current official notice and verify them through the applicable official source or qualified adviser before acting.

### Should I upload every record I collect?

No. Packet inventory is not submission advice. An authorized human should verify current requirements, relevance, privacy, file rules, and the approved channel before any upload or disclosure.

### What does conflict recorded mean?

It means two retained sources do not align on a defined field or period and the difference has been preserved neutrally. It does not decide which source is accurate, controlling, admissible, or persuasive.
