> **Single-report retrieval boundary.** This blank worksheet documents one authorized manual attempt through one named Texas Department of Insurance Title Agent and Escrow Officer report. A displayed row does not establish that the displayed party is the party named in retained correspondence or is involved in a mineral-rights transaction. Keep real names, correspondence, report rows, license details, completed worksheets, and evidence references in an authorized controlled record. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas TDI title agent report retrieval** can preserve a useful source trail when each observation remains attached to one route, one selected report, and one access event. The worksheet records what the selected TDI report displayed. It does not compare the display with a letter, email, offer, closing instruction, website, or person.

That separation matters when a title or escrow party is named near a proposed mineral-rights transaction. The name in correspondence is a private source claim. A row in a TDI report is public source evidence. Neither source, by itself, proves that the two refer to the same party or that the displayed party has a role in the transaction.

## Direct answer

Create one worksheet ID for one authorized manual retrieval attempt. Start at the TDI Title Insurance page, follow the Title Agent and Escrow Officer reports link, select one named report under the source-displayed report group, and record the official route that the browser reached.

Record the selected report title and type, access timestamp and timezone, source-displayed report date or update label when shown, actual filters or selection state, displayed column labels, controlled input reference, controlled evidence pointer, source limitation, and one outcome: `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`.

Stop there. Do not compare the result with correspondence, interpret a license or appointment, identify a transaction party, assess a complaint, judge an offer or buyer, or recommend a closing or transaction action.

## Use one named report and one attempt

The TDI report interface can present more than one report under a report group. Do not combine several report names, filters, outputs, or access events into one worksheet. Record the exact source-displayed report title that was selected and retain a controlled reference to the resulting display or file.

Start a separate worksheet ID or version when any of these changes:

- the official route;
- the selected report title or type;
- the controlled input reference;
- the filter or selection state;
- the report date or update label;
- the access timestamp or timezone;
- the displayed column set;
- the result state; or
- the retained evidence pointer.

This one-attempt rule prevents a later reviewer from treating several different source states as one continuous result. It also keeps an archived report, a current report, a title-agent report, and an escrow-officer report from being merged into an unsupported status history.

It does not prove that a similarly named person or entity in correspondence is the same party. It does not establish transaction involvement, authority, legitimacy, safety, offer quality, or whether anyone should proceed.

## Start from the official TDI title route

The Texas Department of Insurance [Title Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/title/) page currently provides the Title Agent and Escrow Officer reports link. The page says those reports list licensed title agents and escrow officers and instructs a user to select a report under the source-displayed Title Agent / Escrow Officers group.

Those statements support a narrow route record. Preserve the TDI starting page, the final report destination, the report group, the exact selected report title, and the access time. If the source displays a report date, update label, filter, format, or column heading, record it exactly.

Do not add a field that the selected report did not display. Do not use another website, retained correspondence, memory, a search snippet, or a different report to fill a blank. Use `not stated`, `not displayed`, `not applicable`, or `unverified` according to the actual source state.

## Preserve source context without interpreting it

TDI's [overview of title-agent oversight](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/title/overctitleagnt.html) says lists of active title agents and escrow officers are available through the TDI reports site. The same page separately describes licensing and county-appointment conditions for title agents.

Keep those statements separate. A selected report can preserve source-displayed information. This worksheet does not decide what a license label means, whether an appointment applies to a particular county, whether a person may perform a requested task, or whether any regulatory condition has been satisfied for a transaction.

Do not turn a report row into a broader statement such as `approved`, `safe`, `authorized for this closing`, `connected to this offer`, or `verified transaction party`. Those are not attempt-level retrieval observations.

## Preserve three controlled outcomes

Use only these outcomes:

- **Located:** the selected report displayed the controlled search target or retained row reference during the recorded attempt.
- **Not located:** the selected report did not display the controlled search target during that attempt.
- **Unverified:** access, report identity, source date, missing fields, similar names, incomplete evidence, or another recorded limitation prevented either bounded observation.

Do not convert `located` into `licensed for this transaction`, `authorized`, `legitimate`, `safe`, `reputable`, or `approved`. Do not convert `not located` into `unlicensed`, `nonexistent`, `fraudulent`, `illegitimate`, `unauthorized`, or `unsafe`.

The outcome describes one report attempt. It is not a license interpretation, identity match, complaint finding, due-diligence conclusion, or recommendation.

## Keep correspondence and TDI evidence separate

Retained correspondence may supply a reason to perform the lookup, but its real name and details remain in a private controlled record. The public worksheet records only a controlled input pointer. It does not quote the correspondence or reproduce its name, email, phone number, address, offer terms, closing instructions, or attachments.

The TDI report evidence receives a different controlled pointer. Do not combine the correspondence and report into one merged party record. Do not label names as a match or mismatch. Do not infer identity from similar spelling, abbreviations, trade names, addresses, counties, websites, or other fields.

Use the separate [offer sender identity cross-check](/blog/mineral-rights-offer-sender-identity-cross-check/) when an authorized process needs to organize stated sender fields across selected sources. Use the [offer correspondence index](/blog/mineral-rights-offer-correspondence-index/) to inventory communication items. This worksheet performs neither job.

## Exclude property and offer analysis

Do not add a county, tract, survey, abstract, section, block, acreage, interest, depth, formation, substance, lease, unit, well, API number, reservation, or exception to this report-attempt worksheet. Those fields can create an unsupported property connection and belong to separate authorized records.

Do not add price, value, acreage-normalization, offer terms, rankings, scores, red flags, complaints, funding, financial capacity, reputation, buyer quality, or closing conditions. The report retrieval does not establish any of those facts and does not compare one offer with another.

The public template also needs no completed party name, license number, appointment, address, county, report row, correspondence excerpt, property description, signature, result image, or transaction detail. Retain completed evidence only in an authorized system with appropriate access controls.

## Use authorized manual access only

Follow the official site's current navigation and access requirements. This article does not provide a login, submission, order, purchase, contact, complaint, scraping, bulk-download, access-evasion, or rate-limit-workaround procedure.

If the official route changes, preserve the route attempted, access time, redirect or unavailable state, and source limitation. Use `unverified` when the selected report cannot be identified or retained well enough for another authorized reviewer to understand the attempt.

## Stop at authorized-human review

Close the worksheet with the controlled outcome, limitation, unresolved question, and next authorized role. Do not answer the later question inside this retrieval record.

Questions about the meaning of a license, active label, county appointment, title-agent role, escrow-officer role, complaint, authority, regulatory status, legal effect, closing process, or transaction conduct should go to TDI or an appropriately qualified Texas professional. Identity matching, offer analysis, valuation, title review, and transaction decisions require separate purposes, sources, qualifications, and safeguards.

This worksheet documents one manual TDI report retrieval attempt. It does not provide legal, tax, title, appraisal, valuation, investment, financial, compliance, identity-verification, offer, closing, or transaction advice.
