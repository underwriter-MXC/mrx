> **Pre-contact routing boundary.** This blank worksheet organizes a single existing administrative question before any outreach. It does not search for an account; contact an office, contractor, or other person; submit a form or data request; create an account; sign in; upload a record; disclose private data; pay a fee; or interpret an appraisal, tax record, legal instrument, or professional conclusion. A source-displayed destination does not establish identity, ownership, title, acreage, legal-description accuracy, appraisal correctness, market value, tax liability, property-to-well connection, production, offer quality, buyer legitimacy, contractor independence, professional qualification, response time, outcome, legal effect, or transaction suitability. MRX may have an economic interest in a later mineral transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Brazos CAD mineral and industrial account contact** worksheet answers one narrow question: based on current source-displayed role and contact labels, where should a single existing administrative question be routed for a later authorized human review?

The output is not a sent message. It is a source-controlled routing record. Record the route, exact label, access time, controlled reference, neutral question, source limitation, and one of three states: `routed`, `unresolved`, or `authorized-human-stop`.

## Use the six-step route

1. **Open one blank worksheet.** Assign a neutral worksheet ID that contains no owner name, account number, or property detail.
2. **Describe a single existing question.** Copy only the minimum neutral wording needed to classify it. Do not add facts or conclusions.
3. **Open the current source.** Start from the official local district or the source-named contractor page and record the exact URL and access time.
4. **Capture the displayed role.** Preserve the responsibility statement, destination label, and relevant source limitation exactly enough for a later reviewer to reproduce the routing decision.
5. **Store the private reference separately.** Use a controlled pointer to the account notice, correspondence, or retained record. Do not paste private content into the public worksheet.
6. **Choose one bounded state.** Mark `routed`, `unresolved`, or `authorized-human-stop`, then stop. A later authorized person decides whether contact is appropriate.

This workflow should end before a phone call, email, portal submission, registration, upload, payment, or appraisal discussion begins.

## Record only the fields needed to reproduce the route

- **Worksheet ID:** Record a neutral internal label. Do not include an owner name, account number, or property clue.
- **Existing question:** Record one short administrative question. Do not insert a value opinion, legal position, or assumed answer.
- **Source identity:** Record the publisher, page title, URL, access date, and time zone. Do not infer a relationship the source does not display.
- **Displayed route:** Record the exact responsibility statement, role column, or contact label. Do not claim authority over every issue, acceptance, or response.
- **Controlled reference:** Record a nonpublic pointer to the retained notice or correspondence. Do not place the notice, identifier, private data, or screenshot in a public copy.
- **Source limitation:** Record the current disclaimer, login boundary, or unresolved scope. Do not claim completeness, accuracy, qualification, or legal effect.
- **Routing state:** Record `routed`, `unresolved`, or `authorized-human-stop`. Do not use resolved, verified, approved, correct, or complete.
- **Next authorized owner:** Record the role permitted to review the route. Do not turn the field into an instruction to disclose data or contact someone automatically.

Use `not displayed` when a current source lacks a field. Do not fill the gap from memory, a search snippet, a prior-year notice, or another person's account.

## Separate source labels from conclusions

The current [Capitol Appraisal Group mineral interface](https://mineral.cagi.com/Main?modulenum=242) displays a mineral owner-relations route for value or ownership questions, concerns, and updates. It separately displays a corporate route for clients, appraisal, and software services. Preserve the exact label that supports the route. Do not convert a label into legal authority, professional endorsement, a promise to resolve the question, or an instruction to share a notice.

The current [assignment table](https://cagi.com/clients-_-appraisers) includes a Brazos row and separates industrial-appraiser, mineral-appraiser-value, and mineral-analyst-ownership columns. Those are source-displayed role categories. They do not prove that a named person is the correct destination for every account question, that the table is a title record, or that a reviewer has verified the account, owner, appraisal, or legal effect.

The current [client page](https://www.cagi.com/client-base) lists Brazos County Appraisal District. Preserve the page and access time if that relationship is part of the route. Do not infer contract duration, scope, exclusivity, independence, professional qualification, service level, response time, or outcome.

## Keep the local district and contractor rows separate

Create one row for the local district source and another for the source-named contractor when both are relevant. Do not collapse them into a single implied organization.

The [Brazos CAD Property Search](https://esearch.brazoscad.org/) identifies the local appraisal-district destination. It also states that displayed 2026 values are preliminary, that the information is for research, and that legal descriptions and acreage should be verified before legal use. Those limitations continue to apply when a search result or notice generates a later question.

This contact-routing worksheet does not perform the public search. The separate [Brazos CAD mineral account search worksheet](/blog/brazos-cad-mineral-account-search-retrieval-provenance-worksheet/) owns one no-login search attempt, one source-displayed mode, one known criterion, and `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`. This page begins only after an administrative question already exists and stops before contact.

The [Texas Comptroller local-property directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/) distinguishes appraisal-district questions from tax assessor-collector questions. Use that as institutional context, not an owner-specific jurisdiction decision. A question about a tax bill, tax receipt, payment, or tax certificate is not automatically a mineral-appraisal-contractor question.

## Write a neutral question, not a conclusion

A neutral question identifies the source field and the clarification needed without asserting the answer.

Use patterns such as:

- `Which current source-displayed route accepts an administrative question about the label on the retained notice?`
- `Does the current destination page display a route for this question category, or should the worksheet remain unresolved?`
- `Which authorized reviewer should decide whether the controlled reference may be disclosed?`

Avoid statements such as:

- `This account belongs to the owner.`
- `The appraisal is wrong.`
- `The contractor must correct the value.`
- `This role confirms ownership.`
- `The route proves the property is connected to a well.`
- `The value shown is what the minerals are worth.`

Those statements require evidence and professional judgment outside this routing job.

## Use the three routing states precisely

### Routed

Use `routed` only when a current source displays a destination label that matches the neutral administrative question category. Record the supporting source, exact label, access time, limitation, and controlled reference. `Routed` does not mean contacted, received, accepted, assigned, correct, complete, or resolved.

### Unresolved

Use `unresolved` when the current sources are unavailable, stale, inconsistent, incomplete, or too broad to distinguish destinations. Preserve each conflicting label and access attempt. Do not pick the route that sounds most convenient or use an older page to conceal the conflict.

### Authorized-human-stop

Use `authorized-human-stop` before any step that would expose private information, send a message, create an account, sign in, upload a file, submit a request, pay a fee, solve a CAPTCHA, interpret a notice, challenge an appraisal, or request owner-specific legal, tax, valuation, engineering, or transaction advice.

The stop state is a successful boundary, not a failed worksheet. It shows exactly where administrative routing ended and new authority or qualified judgment would begin.

## Protect the underlying record

The public template should remain blank. In the controlled version, use a reference such as `CONTROLLED-NOTICE-REF` rather than copying an owner key, account number, property ID, geographic ID, notice image, owner name, address, email, phone number, legal description, acreage, interest, value, or tax amount.

Do not send the controlled record from this workflow. A later authorized human should verify the destination, decide whether disclosure is necessary, minimize the disclosed fields, use an approved channel, and preserve the resulting correspondence separately.

If the source requires registration or login, record `authorized-human-stop`. Do not create credentials, test an account, upload a notice, or try to reach private content merely to finish the routing worksheet.

## Do not turn appraisal administration into mineral-sale value

The word `value` may appear in a source-displayed role label. That does not authorize a mineral-sale estimate. This worksheet does not analyze production, decline, commodity prices, operator plans, geology, acreage, royalty decimals, cash flow, comparable sales, offers, buyers, taxes, basis, or transaction terms.

An appraisal-system value serves a property-tax administration context. It is not automatically a market offer, owner-specific mineral appraisal, fair-price conclusion, or transaction recommendation. Keep the separate explanation in [Why Doesn't My Texas Mineral Tax Value Match a Sale Estimate?](/blog/why-doesnt-my-texas-mineral-tax-value-match-a-sale-estimate/) when that distinction is the actual question.

## Run the release checklist before a routing record is complete

- a single existing administrative question is recorded without adding facts;
- one neutral worksheet ID contains no private clue;
- each source row includes publisher, page title, exact URL, access date, time, and time zone;
- the exact displayed responsibility statement, role category, or contact label is preserved;
- the private source record is referenced only by a controlled pointer;
- current source limitations and any login boundary are stated;
- district, contractor, tax-office, legal, tax, valuation, and transaction roles are not merged;
- the state is exactly routed, unresolved, or authorized-human-stop;
- no contact, submission, registration, login, upload, payment, bypass, scraping, or bulk collection occurred;
- no identity, ownership, title, acreage, legal-description, appraisal, tax, value, production, offer, professional, legal, or transaction conclusion appears.

If any item fails, keep the worksheet unresolved or stop for an authorized human.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a contractor route prove who has authority over my mineral account?

No. It preserves a current source-displayed destination label for one neutral administrative question. It does not establish contractual scope, legal authority, professional qualification, ownership, title, appraisal correctness, or a promised response.

### Should I place my owner key or account number in the public worksheet?

No. Keep identifiers, notices, screenshots, contact details, and completed routing records in an authorized controlled system. The reusable public worksheet and images remain blank.

### Can this worksheet tell me whether a mineral appraisal is correct?

No. It does not evaluate appraisal methods, inputs, values, notices, protests, tax liability, mineral market value, or an owner-specific conclusion. It only preserves a pre-contact route and its source limits.

### What if the current sources point to different destinations?

Preserve each source and access time, describe the conflict neutrally, and mark `unresolved` or `authorized-human-stop`. Do not select a destination by guessing or merge role labels into a conclusion.

### Does routed mean the office or contractor received my question?

No. `Routed` means only that the worksheet identified a source-supported destination before contact. This workflow does not send a message, submit a form, create an account, upload a record, or predict a response or result.
