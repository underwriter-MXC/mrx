> **Public retrieval boundary.** This article records one authorized manual attempt through the Texas Railroad Commission's public **Production by Lease** query path. It does not interpret production or connect a displayed result to a well, operator, property, tract, owner, title, payment, acreage, decimal, tax, reserve, forecast, value, offer, legal position, compliance finding, or transaction. It does not provide legal, title, land, tax, accounting, appraisal, engineering, regulatory, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC Production by Lease retrieval** worksheet answers one narrow question: exactly how was one authorized manual attempt performed through the source-labeled **Production by Lease** path? Record the official route, exact labels actually used, controlled criteria reference, access time and timezone, displayed state, retained evidence, source limits, and one bounded retrieval status.

Use only **located**, **not located**, or **unverified**. Those terms describe the attempt. They do not decide what a production figure means, whether a lease is connected to a private tract, who owns an interest, whether royalties were calculated correctly, or what mineral rights are worth.

## Start from the official query directory

The Texas RRC's [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory identifies **Production Reports Query (Form PR)** as an official research route. The linked [Production Report Queries](https://webapps.rrc.texas.gov/PR/publicQueriesMainAction.do) application currently presents three distinct choices:

- **Production by Lease**;
- **Production by Filing Operator**; and
- **Production by Operator of Record (as shown on RRC Form P-4)**.

This worksheet owns the first path only. Copy the exact route and label displayed when the attempt begins. Do not silently substitute a filing-operator or operator-of-record search because its inputs appear related. A route switch changes the reader job and the meaning of the retrieval record.

The official directory and application establish where the public query begins and which choices the source displays. They do not establish a private-property connection, ownership, title, payment entitlement, production attribution, reserve estimate, mineral value, or legal conclusion.

## Give one attempt a neutral header

Assign a neutral attempt ID such as `TX-RRC-PBL-01`. The ID labels a retrieval session, not a lease, property, well, operator, owner, royalty account, production conclusion, or transaction.

Record:

- the Research Queries directory URL and the launched application URL;
- the exact application and **Production by Lease** labels displayed;
- access date, time, and timezone;
- preparer role and worksheet version;
- purpose, limited to one manual retrieval attempt;
- the controlled location for actual criteria and retained evidence;
- the displayed navigation, validation, result, empty-result, error, timeout, or access state; and
- the source limitations preserved with the attempt.

Use a new ID for every rerun. Do not overwrite an earlier attempt because a criterion, interface, validation message, result, source notice, or access condition changed. A preserved sequence shows what was retrievable at each recorded time.

## Select only Production by Lease

Open the official application and record the source-labeled route selected. If **Production by Lease** is unavailable, renamed, redirected, or replaced by a materially different workflow, stop and mark the attempt **unverified**. Do not continue through another path merely to obtain a result.

The Filing Operator and Operator of Record paths are separate because the source presents them separately. This worksheet also stays separate from the RRC Production Data Query, New Lease IDs, proration, Wellbore, and broad production-record locator resources. Each route needs its own purpose, inputs, timestamp, displayed state, limitations, and result description.

That separation prevents a later reviewer from mistaking evidence from one system or query mode for evidence from another.

## Record the visible criteria labels actually used

The current **Production by Lease** screen displays criteria labeled **Lease Type**, **District**, **RRC Identifier**, and **Prod Month Range**. Copy labels exactly as displayed at access time. Do not assume those labels, defaults, choices, or validation rules will remain unchanged.

For each used criterion, preserve:

- the exact source label;
- any default visibly presented;
- whether the preparer changed it;
- a controlled reference to the actual value;
- the action taken immediately before the displayed state; and
- the retained-evidence reference for that state.

Public placeholders can include `[controlled lease type]`, `[controlled district]`, `[controlled RRC identifier]`, `[controlled production-month range]`, and `[controlled retained evidence]`. Do not publish actual identifiers, criteria, result rows, screenshots, account details, private correspondence, or owner records in a reusable worksheet.

## Preserve the displayed state without interpreting it

A retrieval may display the expected form, a navigation step, a required-field message, a validation state, a result list, an empty result, an error, a timeout, a maintenance notice, or another access boundary. Record what appeared and what action immediately preceded it.

Useful neutral descriptions include:

- official Production Report Queries entry surface displayed;
- Production by Lease path selected;
- Lease Type, District, RRC Identifier, and Prod Month Range controls displayed;
- controlled criteria entered or selected;
- source validation or required-field message displayed;
- result page or result list displayed;
- controlled target displayed or not displayed;
- empty, error, timeout, unavailable, or access-boundary state displayed; or
- source state could not be verified.

A form that was never submitted is not a no-match result. A submitted attempt with no visible match is not proof that no record exists. Preserve the route, controlled criteria reference, timestamp, action, and evidence reference that belong to the state.

Do not interpret displayed volumes, months, statuses, identifiers, or party names inside this worksheet. A later question about production history or a private interest requires a separately scoped evidence review.

## Keep current-query and historical-data routes separate

The RRC's [Oil and Gas Production Data](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/production-data/) page describes official production-data resources. Its [About Oil and Gas Data Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/about-oil-gas-data-queries/) page provides context about the agency's query systems and their data scope.

Use those pages to record source context and limits only. Do not merge a result from another production-data route into the **Production by Lease** attempt. If another official source is needed, give it a separate retrieval record and preserve why it was consulted.

The existence of another data surface does not prove that either route is complete for a private purpose. It also does not authorize an assumption about how records should be joined across leases, wells, operators, properties, owners, or time periods.

## Do not automate or bulk-retrieve

Use the public application manually and within the conditions displayed by the source. This worksheet does not authorize scraping, scripted navigation, repeated automated polling, bulk collection, session manipulation, validation bypass, access-control evasion, or attempts to reach restricted functions.

If the source requires a capability that is unavailable, presents a material access boundary, or changes the route so the attempt can no longer be reproduced, record **unverified**. Do not work around the condition merely to advance the worksheet.

## Retain evidence in a controlled location

The reusable worksheet should store a controlled reference, not the underlying evidence. A retained-evidence record can identify:

- attempt ID and worksheet version;
- authorized repository, case system, or evidence-vault location;
- capture, note, or result filename;
- capture timestamp and timezone;
- file SHA-256 or another integrity reference;
- source URL and exact route label;
- controlled criteria reference;
- access role and retention rule; and
- whether a later reviewer independently repeated the attempt.

Do not publish actual criteria, result rows, screenshots, party names, property identifiers, owner records, royalty statements, correspondence, contact information, credentials, cookies, session data, or internal case notes. Share underlying evidence only with authorized recipients through an approved channel and for a stated purpose.

## Assign only a bounded retrieval status

Choose one status after the attempt:

1. **Located:** the recorded route exposed the specific controlled target sought.
2. **Not located:** the recorded attempt did not expose that target using the stated route, criteria, source state, and access time.
3. **Unverified:** the route, exact labels, controlled criteria, displayed state, retained evidence, or source limitation was not captured well enough to reproduce the attempt.

`Located` does not authenticate a record or connect it to a private interest. `Not located` does not prove absence. `Unverified` is the correct status when the evidence trail or source access is incomplete.

End with one neutral next question. For example: should an authorized reviewer repeat the same route with a corrected controlled criterion, preserve a changed validation state, or open a separately named official data source under a new attempt ID? Do not end with a production, property, ownership, title, payment, acreage, tax, reserve, forecast, valuation, offer, legal, compliance, or transaction conclusion.

## Protect the mineral owner's information

Store real identifiers, search criteria, results, screenshots, property records, royalty records, and correspondence in an access-controlled location. Share only records you are authorized to provide and disclose why each record is requested.

If MRX or an affiliate could acquire an interest, that economic relationship should be stated clearly before a transaction agreement is signed. A provenance worksheet improves reproducibility; it does not make MRX a regulator, title examiner, attorney, accountant, engineer, appraiser, investment adviser, or neutral fiduciary.

## Final retrieval check

Before closing the attempt, confirm that the worksheet preserves the official directory and application routes, the exact **Production by Lease** selection, exact visible criteria labels actually used, access time and timezone, controlled criteria reference, displayed navigation, validation, result, empty-result, error, or access state, retained evidence, source limitations, bounded status, and one neutral next question. If one item is missing, use **unverified** until the record is complete.

Stop there. The next step may be another official-source retrieval or an appropriately scoped professional review. It is not a production, property, ownership, title, payment, acreage, tax, reserve, forecast, valuation, offer, legal, compliance, or transaction conclusion.
