> **Public retrieval boundary.** This article records one authorized manual attempt through the Texas Railroad Commission's Imaged Records **Dry Hole Files** profile. It does not interpret a document or determine record completeness, permit status, plugging, directional-survey facts, well identity or status, production, reserves, safety, environmental condition, compliance, property connection, ownership, title, payment, acreage, value, offer quality, legal effect, tax, or transaction outcome. It does not provide legal, title, land, regulatory, environmental, geological, engineering, tax, accounting, appraisal, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC dry hole file retrieval** worksheet preserves how one manual source attempt was made. It identifies the official route, retains the displayed family and profile identity, timestamps the attempt, points to controlled criteria, records the submit method and access or result state, preserves any document-link or viewer state, retains an evidence reference, attaches source-coverage notes, and assigns one bounded outcome.

Use only **located**, **not located**, or **unverified**. These terms describe one retrieval attempt. They do not convert a profile label, document link, image, source value, or empty result into a conclusion about a well, property, owner, regulator action, compliance issue, valuation, offer, or transaction.

## Begin with the official Imaged Records route

Start with the Texas RRC [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory and preserve its official **Oil & Gas Imaged Records Menu** route. Record the directory URL, displayed source label, access time, and menu URL before attempting to open a profile.

The RRC [Imaged Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/imaged-records/) page lists **Dry Hole Files** separately from Oil & Gas Well Records (Potential files) and Oil & Gas Well Logs. That separation matters. Record the family and profile that the official route actually identifies; do not substitute a nearby collection because its controls or records appear similar.

This is a one-attempt manual record, not an automated research workflow. Do not scrape the source, retrieve records in volume, create a repeated polling process, bypass an access control, automate CAPTCHA handling, or work around validation, session, timeout, or viewer states. When the official path cannot be completed as authorized, document the stop point and use **unverified**.

## Preserve the Dry Hole Files profile identity

The official [Imaged Records Query Menu](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/imaged-records/imaged-records-menu/) identifies **Dry Hole Files** and links the profile route with `profileId=9`. Preserve the official menu URL, displayed profile label, link destination, and access timestamp. Treat the profile ID as route provenance, not as evidence about a well or document.

The menu describes coverage from 2000 forward and notes that older District 9 files are added as time permits. The Imaged Records page says the dry-hole imaging project is ongoing and not all files are available for viewing. Record those statements with the access date. Do not rewrite them as a guarantee that every expected record is online or that an empty result is complete.

| Source route or family | Boundary for this worksheet |
|---|---|
| Dry Hole Files profileId 9 | Preserve one manual attempt, its source-displayed states, and its evidence trail. |
| Oil and Gas Potential profile | Separate imaged-record profile; do not merge potential-file links or `FILM YR` states. |
| Well Log profile | Separate imaged-record profile; do not copy log images or viewer conclusions. |
| Drilling Permit Query | Separate electronic permit route with its own coverage and result states. |
| Completions Query | Separate online packet route; do not treat a dry-hole profile state as a completion result. |
| Wellbore, P-4, P-17, production, GIS, EDMS, or hearing sources | Separate systems requiring separate attempt records. |

The profile name is a routing fact. It does not establish that a particular well is a dry hole, that every expected page is available, that a document belongs to a reader's mineral interest, or that a permit, plugging report, or survey is current, complete, or legally sufficient.

## Record the current access state truthfully

During preparation of this article, a direct request to the official Dry Hole Files profile route returned HTTP `403`. No profile page was captured, no search form was verified, no criteria were submitted, and no query completed. The only defensible outcome for that attempt is **unverified**.

Do not relabel an access response as a search result. HTTP `403`, a blocked page, an error, a timeout, a validation failure, an interrupted session, a CAPTCHA, an unavailable viewer, a default screen, or an incomplete attempt does not show that a record is absent. Preserve the exact stop point, timestamp, route, response state, and evidence reference without attempting a bypass.

## Create the attempt header before entering criteria

Assign a neutral attempt ID that reveals no private property, party, ownership, or transaction information. Then record:

- the official Research Queries, Imaged Records, and menu URLs;
- the displayed Dry Hole Files label and profile ID;
- access date, clock time, and explicit timezone;
- the authorized manual access method;
- the displayed criteria labels, if the profile becomes accessible;
- a controlled criteria-set reference and exact entered or selected values in that controlled record;
- the manual submit method;
- the displayed access, validation, submission, result, or interruption state;
- any document-link, image, download, or viewer state actually displayed;
- the retained evidence location and access restriction;
- source coverage, imaging-status, and retrieval-date notes; and
- the transfer destination, recipient limits, unresolved questions, and recheck requirement.

Do not publish API numbers, districts, field identifiers, lease names or numbers, well numbers, operator names, locations, date ranges, images, or party names merely because a source accepts or displays them. Keep necessary values in an authorized controlled location and let the worksheet point to that record.

## Keep source-described content separate from retrieved content

The Imaged Records page describes images of dry-hole records from 2000 to the current period as including certain drilling permits for wells permitted before May 1, 2005, plugging reports, and directional surveys when required. This is source-described collection scope. It does not mean that every category will appear for every profile search or that a displayed image proves a substantive fact.

Record the source description separately from the attempt result. If a link appears, preserve its source-displayed label, link state, viewer state, and evidence reference. Do not interpret whether the document is complete, current, accepted, associated with the intended well, or relevant to a private mineral interest.

The official [Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) page distinguishes dry-hole records from completed-well records and provides historical request context. Use that information only to preserve route boundaries and source notes. Do not silently move to a completed-well, permit, completion, wellbore, or production system under the same attempt ID.

## Record document-link and viewer states independently

If the profile becomes accessible, keep each state separate:

- **Access state:** whether the official profile route loaded and could be used manually.
- **Criteria state:** whether the source accepted the recorded criteria.
- **Result state:** whether the completed attempt displayed responsive rows, an attributable empty result, or an inconclusive state.
- **Document-link state:** whether a responsive document link was presented.
- **Viewer state:** whether an image opened, remained unavailable, or could not be verified.
- **Evidence state:** where the authorized source-state evidence is retained.

The [Well Records FAQs](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/about-us/faqs/oil-gas-faq/well-records-faqs/) provide image-format, film-cycle, and viewer context for well records. Preserve only the context that matches a source-displayed state. A viewer failure does not prove that the underlying document is missing, and an image opening does not prove that it is complete or connected to the intended property.

## Apply fail-closed outcome rules

Use **located** only when a completed, attributable manual attempt displays responsive material in the Dry Hole Files profile. Located does not mean complete, correct, current, relevant, verified, or legally sufficient.

Use **not located** only when all of these are true:

1. the official Dry Hole Files profile loaded;
2. the displayed criteria labels were recorded;
3. the controlled criteria were accepted;
4. the manual submission completed;
5. the source displayed an empty result attributable to those criteria; and
6. no block, error, timeout, validation, session, CAPTCHA, viewer, or interpretation issue makes the result inconclusive.

Use **unverified** for every other state. The current HTTP `403` attempt is unverified. Do not retry repeatedly, change systems, weaken criteria, or use a neighboring profile merely to obtain a more satisfying label.

| Official route | Research Queries, Imaged Records, menu, and profile URLs |
| Profile identity | Dry Hole Files and profileId 9 as displayed by the official menu |
| Access timestamp | Date, clock time, and timezone |
| Coverage note | Source wording, access date, and ongoing-imaging limitation |
| Criteria labels | Source-displayed labels actually presented |
| Criteria reference | Controlled record containing exact entered or selected values |
| Submit method | Manual control used, if the profile became accessible |

| Document-link state | Not presented, presented, opened, unavailable, or unclear |
| Viewer state | Not attempted, opened, unavailable, blocked, or unclear |
| Bounded outcome | Located, not located, or unverified |
| Retained evidence reference | Screenshot, response note, controlled file, or other authorized reference |
| Adjacent routes excluded | Separate systems not searched under this attempt |
| Transfer note | Recipient, limits, unresolved questions, and recheck requirement |

## Final quality check

Before closing the record, confirm that:

- the attempt started from the official Imaged Records route and preserved the Dry Hole Files profile identity;
- the timestamp includes a timezone;
- source labels and any displayed criteria labels are exact;
- actual criteria values remain controlled and are not unnecessarily published;
- HTTP `403`, blocked, error, timeout, validation, default, session, CAPTCHA, and viewer states remain **unverified**;
- **not located** is used only for a completed empty result attributable to accepted recorded criteria;
- document-link, viewer, evidence, coverage, and transfer states are recorded separately;
- adjacent Potential, Well Log, permit, completion, wellbore, production, GIS, EDMS, and hearing routes were not merged;
- no automation, bulk retrieval, repeated-query workflow, bypass, CAPTCHA handling, or workaround occurred; and
- no document interpretation or well, property, ownership, production, environmental, compliance, value, offer, legal, tax, or transaction conclusion was added.

If any item fails, keep the attempt **unverified**. The worksheet succeeds when it preserves what happened, including a blocked or incomplete attempt, without expanding a retrieval event into a technical, property, regulatory, or business conclusion.
