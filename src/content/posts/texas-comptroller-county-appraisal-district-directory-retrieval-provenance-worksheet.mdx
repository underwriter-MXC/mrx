> **Directory-only boundary.** This blank worksheet documents one authorized manual attempt through the official Texas Comptroller county directory and one selected county page. A displayed appraisal-district entry does not establish identity, authenticity, completeness, currency, certification, jurisdiction, service scope, property connection, title, ownership, mineral connection, appraisal, tax treatment, value, or legal effect. Keep completed worksheets, screenshots, contact values, and retained evidence in an authorized controlled system. A **Texas county appraisal district directory retrieval** can preserve where a contact entry came from without turning that entry into a property or tax record. The worksheet records one official route, one county page, the labels displayed during one access event, and what prevented a bounded result.

That separation matters when an inherited mineral-rights file includes a tax notice, county reference, or appraisal-district name. The Comptroller directory can help preserve the official contact route. It does not identify the owner of a mineral interest, search a local appraisal roll, explain a tax bill, or decide whether a contact or taxing unit is relevant to a tract.

## Direct answer

Create one worksheet ID for one authorized manual attempt. Open the Texas Comptroller [Local Property Appraisal and Tax Information directory](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/), select one source-displayed county code and name, and preserve the official county-page route.

Record the access timestamp and timezone; exact `Appraisal District` and `Last Updated` labels; displayed labels for the chief appraiser, taxpayer liaison, phone, toll-free number, fax, email, website, mailing address, and street address; displayed active-taxing-unit codes and names; a controlled retained-evidence pointer; a source limitation; and one outcome: `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`.

Stop there. Do not follow the local website into a property search, record request, appraisal review, tax inquiry, identity match, or transaction analysis. Do not call, email, submit, sign in, purchase, bypass, scrape, or bulk collect under this workflow.

## Use one official index and one county page

The Comptroller directory states that the agency does not have access to local property appraisal or tax information and directs most local appraisal and property-tax questions to the county appraisal district or tax assessor-collector. It describes the directory as contact information for those offices plus the taxing units each serves.

That statement defines the job. Use the statewide directory only to preserve the official directory trail. Do not treat it as a statewide property, ownership, appraisal, or tax database.

Start a new worksheet ID or version when any of these changes:

- the official index route;
- the selected county code or name;
- the county-page route;
- the access timestamp or timezone;
- the source-displayed section or update label;
- the displayed contact or address field set;
- the active-taxing-unit display;
- the retained-evidence pointer;
- the source limitation; or
- the controlled outcome.

Do not merge entries from multiple counties, local websites, search engines, tax offices, appraisal systems, or retained documents. A later reviewer should be able to see which exact official page produced each observation.

## Preserve source-displayed fields without extending them

A representative official county page, such as the Comptroller's [Montgomery County directory page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/county-directory/montgomery.php), shows the structure the worksheet is designed to preserve. The page includes an `Appraisal District` section, a `Last Updated` label, contact-field labels, mailing and street address sections, and an `Active Taxing Units` list.

Preserve the labels and values as source-displayed observations in the controlled record. Do not normalize a blank field into `none`, infer that a listed website is reachable, treat a displayed person as the current officeholder, or assume that the active-taxing-unit list is complete for a later purpose.

The directory says it is periodically updated with information reported by appraisal districts and tax offices. That is a source limitation. A displayed date or field does not authorize this worksheet to certify the entry or guarantee that later contact information remains unchanged.

## Preserve three controlled outcomes

Use only these outcomes:

- **Located:** the official selected-county page displayed the bounded appraisal-district directory section during the documented attempt.
- **Not located:** the recorded official page did not display the bounded section during that attempt.
- **Unverified:** route identity, essential labels, access state, evidence retention, or another limitation prevented either bounded observation.

Do not convert `located` into `accurate`, `complete`, `current`, `verified contact`, `correct jurisdiction`, `serves this property`, `owns this record`, or `can answer this mineral question`.

Do not convert `not located` into `no appraisal district`, `no tax office`, `no taxing unit`, `no property record`, or `no mineral interest`. One page, label, route, or access event may be incomplete or unavailable.

## Stop before property or tax-record research

The Comptroller's [Property Tax System Basics](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/basics.php) explains that local appraisal districts administer property appraisal functions and directs local-process questions to the local district. This worksheet does not perform those functions and does not contact the district.

- enter an owner, heir, estate, address, parcel, legal description, or account number;
- retrieve a tax bill, certificate, receipt, protest, exemption, valuation, or appraisal record;

- bypass a CAPTCHA, rate limit, access control, or terms; or

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the directory search mineral or property records?

No. The directory provides office contact and served-taxing-unit information. A local appraisal or tax system is a separate source with a different purpose, fields, access rules, and evidentiary limits.

### Can a displayed taxing-unit code be connected to my tract?

Not in this workflow. Preserve the code and name only as directory fields. Connecting a taxing unit to a property or mineral interest requires separate source evidence and qualified review.

### What if a contact field is blank?

Record the source display as blank or not stated. Do not fill the gap from memory, a search result, a third-party directory, or a different county page.

### What if the official page changes after I record it?

Use a new worksheet version or ID for the later access event. Preserve both access times, routes, displayed labels, retained pointers, and limitations without declaring which version is legally or factually controlling.

### Can I use the local website linked from the directory?

The link can be preserved as a displayed directory field. Opening it for a property search, record request, contact, login, or submission is outside this worksheet and requires a separate authorized workflow.
