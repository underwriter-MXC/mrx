> **Public-record retrieval boundary.** This article records one Texas RRC P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query attempt. It does not interpret a P-4, gatherer, purchaser, operator, P-5 number, district, lease number, gas-well ID, field, well type, product, result row, or record. It does not establish property connection, title, ownership, acreage, tract or unit inclusion, lease effect, payee status, royalty share, decimal, payment responsibility, entitlement, compliance, completeness, accuracy, transportation, sale, production, cash flow, value, price, offer quality, or a transaction result. It does not provide legal, title, land, tax, accounting, appraisal, engineering, geological, reserve, investment, or financial advice. MRX may have an economic interest in a later transaction; when that applies, the buyer relationship is disclosed in writing before an agreement is signed.

A **Texas RRC P-4 gatherer/purchaser query retrieval** worksheet answers one narrow question: exactly how was one official P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query attempted? Record the entry route, source-displayed criteria, navigation steps, result-header state, result-row locator, access timestamp, source limitation, retained reference, and one bounded retrieval status.

Use only `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`. Those terms describe the query attempt. They do not interpret a designation, establish a company relationship, or connect a displayed result to private property.

## Start with the named official route

The Railroad Commission of Texas [Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) directory lists the P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query as a named Oil & Gas route. Its [Oil & Gas Data Query menu](https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/ewaMain.do) presents that route separately from production, wellbore, severance, proration, organization, inactive-well, orphan-well, drilling-permit, inspection, and violation queries.

Open the named [P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query](https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/gathererPurchaserQueryAction.do) and preserve the complete HTTPS address. Do not write only `RRC search` or merge this route with a Wellbore, production, drilling-permit, or P-5 query.

A reproducible attempt distinguishes:

- the Research Queries directory used as the entry point;
- the Oil & Gas Data Query menu;
- the P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query criteria screen;
- the result list reached after submission;
- any displayed next route opened from a result; and
- any authorized screenshot or retained reference created during the attempt.

The RRC directory says online query data is continually updated, informational, non-authoritative, and without legal force or effect. Record that limitation with the access time. Do not turn a result into a claim that the online route is complete, current for every purpose, or legally controlling.

For broad orientation, use [Texas Railroad Commission: How to Use Public Records to Understand Your Mineral Rights](/blog/texas-railroad-commission-how-to-use-public-records-to-understand-your-mineral-rights/). This worksheet owns the smaller reproducibility record for one P-4 query execution.

## Create an attempt header before searching

Give the attempt a neutral ID such as `TX-P4-RET-01`. The ID labels a query session, not a property, owner, company conclusion, or transaction.

Record these fields before entering criteria:

- **Attempt ID:** a stable internal label;
- **Purpose:** `public P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query retrieval only`;
- **Official entry route:** the complete address opened;
- **Query-page label:** copied exactly as displayed;
- **Access date and time:** when the criteria screen was viewed;
- **Timezone:** the timezone attached to that timestamp;
- **Prepared by:** a role or controlled identifier;
- **Worksheet version:** the version used for this attempt; and
- **Source limitation:** the source's authority, update, access, coverage, or display note.

Create a new attempt for a later rerun. Do not overwrite an earlier criteria snapshot, result state, timestamp, or retained reference merely because the interface or displayed data changed.

## Capture each used criterion by its displayed label

The RRC's [About Oil & Gas Data Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/about-oil-gas-data-queries/) says the P-4 route can be searched by well type, district, oil lease number or gas-well ID, field, operator, gatherer or purchaser, gatherer/purchaser P-5 number, or gatherer product. The current [P-4 criteria screen](https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/gathererPurchaserQueryAction.do) displays those categories through its own current controls.

Those are locator fields, not conclusions. Create one row for every field used:

- **Criterion label:** copied as displayed;
- **Entry method:** typed value, selected option, helper, or left unused;
- **Controlled value reference:** the entered or selected value stored appropriately;
- **Format shown by source:** split boxes, punctuation, option label, or leading characters;
- **Used in submission:** `yes` or `no`; and
- **Capture note:** any displayed instruction or default that affected the entry.

Avoid a summary such as `searched for a purchaser`. Preserve each label and value separately. If a control remained at its default, record `unused` or the exact displayed default rather than inventing an entry.

## Record the submission and navigation path

After submitting criteria, preserve only the sequence that actually occurred. A bounded path can look like:

`official directory → query menu → P-4 query → criteria submitted → result list → displayed next route`

For each step, record:

- the displayed page or link label;
- the route or controlled route reference;
- the action taken;
- the access time and timezone;
- whether the page loaded, changed, timed out, or showed no rows; and
- the next step actually selected.

Do not reconstruct a route from memory. If the session ended, an error appeared, or the interface changed, preserve that event and use `not located` or `unverified` for the narrow retrieval fact.

## Keep result headers and rows separate

The RRC describes a P-4 as a Producer's Transportation Authority and Certificate of Compliance on which a lease designates a company that will transport product off the lease and a company that will purchase product first. That explanation identifies the source's terminology. It does not prove a private contract, transportation activity, sale, payment, responsibility, property connection, or owner entitlement.

Record the result state separately from individual rows:

- **Criteria echoed by the result page:** exact labels and controlled values;
- **Displayed result range or count:** copied as shown, if present;
- **Display-limit message:** copied or accurately paraphrased, if shown or applicable;
- **Sort field and direction:** only when selected or displayed;
- **Page and page-size state:** the page or control actually reviewed;
- **Result-page access time:** with timezone; and
- **Result-page message or limitation:** copied or accurately paraphrased.

Then copy only fields actually observed, and keep each label attached to its value. A row might preserve a displayed district, lease, field, operator, gatherer/purchaser, type, or product label without deciding what that label means beyond the source screen. Use `not displayed` when the source does not show a field. Similar names, numbers, or labels are not interchangeable.

## Treat a different route as a new retrieval step

If a result exposes a new link, tab, record channel, or document reference, create a separate step with:

- originating attempt ID and result-row reference;
- displayed link or tab label;
- destination route or controlled reference;
- destination page label;
- new record class or fields displayed;
- access timestamp and timezone;
- retained reference location; and
- `located`, `not located`, or `unverified`.

Do not merge every linked page into one generic company or lease record. Do not infer a property relationship from matching operator, lease, county, field, product, company, or P-5 labels. That downstream question belongs to an authorized review and remains outside this worksheet.

## Keep P-4, production, and operator-name work separate

The query menu exposes separate P-4, production, Wellbore, and Organization routes. This worksheet does not retrieve production periods or volumes, interpret a well, establish a company relationship, or reconcile a displayed name across records.

Use explicit source-role labels:

- `public research directory`;
- `Oil & Gas Data Query menu`;
- `P-4 Gatherer/Purchaser Query criteria screen`;
- `P-4 query result list`;
- `displayed next route or record channel`; or
- `authorized retained query reference`.

The [Texas mineral production record locator](/blog/how-to-build-a-texas-mineral-production-record-locator-sheet/) owns production-period retrieval. The [operator-name change log](/blog/mineral-rights-operator-name-change-log/) owns a dated comparison of displayed names. Preserve the boundary even when a result contains a name or another route is available.

## Use only bounded retrieval statuses

Keep the status vocabulary intentionally small:

- **Located:** the recorded official query exposed an identified result reference.
- **Not located:** the recorded attempt did not expose the sought result.
- **Unverified:** a label, identifier, route, or result appeared, but the narrow retrieval fact or relationship was not established.

`Located` does not mean connected, applicable, complete, accurate, current, active, legally effective, contractually binding, paid, productive, or valuable. `Not located` does not mean nonexistent. `Unverified` preserves uncertainty without alleging an error or defect.

Turn gaps into neutral retrieval questions:

- Which criterion may have narrowed this attempt?
- Did the result header echo every criterion as entered?
- Did a display limit or page-size setting constrain what was reviewed?
- Which result page and sort state were examined?
- Does the row expose a separate official route or record channel?
- Which official record-access channel may hold the record class sought?
- What authority, update, access, or coverage limitation does the source state?

Do not convert those questions into statements about ownership, title, acreage, lease effect, payment, compliance, transportation, sale, production, development, reserves, or value.

## Preserve an authorized result reference

The live query and a retained object are different evidence. For every authorized screenshot, export, or file, record:

- attempt ID;
- official route and navigation path;
- criteria snapshot;
- result-header and display state;
- result-row or next-route reference;
- access timestamp and timezone;
- controlled storage location;
- file hash when your records process uses one; and
- handling note for identifiers, names, addresses, legal descriptions, account data, or other sensitive information.

Do not publish an actual lease identifier, gas-well ID, owner record, private document, or property-specific result in a reusable example. A controlled reference proves only what was retained for the recorded attempt.

If the online query is insufficient, the Commission's [Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) page identifies separate record channels and says the RRC has limited authority over lease, royalty, financing, investment, and property-rights matters. Record the next route as a question or new attempt; do not claim the online result is the complete agency record.

## Final quality check

Before closing the worksheet, confirm that:

- the exact official directory, menu, and P-4 query routes are named;
- every submitted criterion preserves its source label and controlled value;
- the navigation path records only steps that occurred;
- result-header state, result rows, and next routes remain separate;
- result range, display limit, sorting, pagination, and page size are recorded only when displayed or used;
- the access timestamp, source limitation, and retained reference are attached;
- every status is only located, not located, or unverified;
- no row interprets a P-4, gatherer, purchaser, operator, P-5 number, lease, field, well type, product, or result;
- no row establishes property connection, ownership, title, acreage, unit inclusion, lease effect, payee status, payment responsibility, entitlement, compliance, completeness, or accuracy; and
- no row infers transportation, sale, production, cash flow, value, price, offer quality, or a transaction result.

A strong provenance worksheet is narrow by design. It lets an authorized reviewer understand and repeat the recorded P-4 query attempt. It does not decide what a designation or record means.
