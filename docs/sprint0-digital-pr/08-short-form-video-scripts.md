## V-8 — "What is a mineral right?" (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

**Runtime:** 51 seconds
**Hook (0-3s):** "What is a mineral right?"
**Body (3-46s):**

```
A mineral right is a property interest that gives the holder
a share of subsurface production. It is separate from surface
rights.

Quick vocabulary:
- Mineral interest (MI): ownership of the share.
- Royalty interest (NRI): the share paid to the owner.
- Working interest (WI): the operator's share.
- Division order: the document that says who gets paid.
- Lease: the agreement that lets the operator drill.
```
