## Pitch patterns that are explicitly **prohibited**

These are the patterns that look like pitches but violate policy. They are listed here so `mrx_seo_pr` can recognize and refuse them.

- "Best mineral rights buyers" listicle pitches — never accept.
- "We will beat any offer" framing in any subject or body — never send.
- "Highest price guaranteed" — never send.
- "USPAP-certified" or "certified appraiser" framing — never send (compliance rule 2).
- Comparative pitches that name a specific competitor — never send (compliance rule 6, `compliance/named-competitors.json`).
- Any pitch that requires payment to be considered — never engage (G-08).
- Any pitch that demands a dofollow backlink as a condition of editorial coverage — never engage (G-08; FTC endorsement guides).
