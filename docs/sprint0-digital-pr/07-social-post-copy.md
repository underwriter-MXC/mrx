### X-5 — Q&A thread (4 posts)

```
1/ "What's a mineral right?" It's a property interest that gives the holder a share of subsurface production. Separate from surface rights.

2/ Two main flavors:
- Mineral interest (MI): ownership of the share.
- Royalty interest (NRI): the share paid to the owner, free of production costs.

3/ Operators report what you own in a "division order." If you cannot find yours, that is the first document to ask for.

4/ Free primer: https://mineralrightsxchange.com/learning-center/
```

### LI-3 — Methodology (1,100 chars)

```
How does the mineral-rights industry frame value? Three drivers
show up in most public sources:

1. Production history. What has the well or unit actually
   produced in the last 12-24 months, by month, and how is
   that production trending?

2. Decline curve. Most modern wells follow a steep early
   decline and then a long tail. The shape of that tail is
   what most of the buyer's math is doing in the background.

3. Operator reliability. Who actually produces, who pays on
   time, and who pays the correct division-order share.

4. Net royalty acres. The owner-side math starts here. NMA =
   acres × NRI. Everything else is operator-side or
   engineering-side.

The full educational methodology is at
https://mineralrightsxchange.com/methodology/ — this is not a
binding appraisal, not a regulated valuation, and not a
guaranteed value. For decisions specific to your situation,
the right professionals are an attorney, a CPA, and a reservoir
engineer in the relevant state.
```
