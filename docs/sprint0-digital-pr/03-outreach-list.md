## Tier 6 — Earned-media wire / press-release editorial

Press releases are **not** for SEO. They are for material news. MRX does not currently have news of the caliber a release editor accepts. This tier stays empty until MRX has:

- A formally launched program (e.g., a free Texas Landowner Workshop series)
- A new educational resource of public-record interest (e.g., a state-by-state inheritance guide)
- A partnership with a licensed-credentialed organization (university extension, state bar section)

**Do not pitch press releases for**: "we exist," "we are transparent," "we are a different kind of buyer." Those are advertorial, not editorial, and they violate the no-superlative rule.

## Compliance posture for every outreach

- No fabricated statistics, no customer counts, no "trusted by" claims.
- No guarantees, no superlatives, no "best mineral" framing.
- No comparative attacks on competitors.
- No individualized legal/tax/valuation advice, ever.
- All citations point to the canonical NAP (`https://mineralrightsxchange.com`, `underwriter@mineralrightsxchange.com`).
- The spokesperson for MRX is identified as an MRX spokesperson or as "the editorial team"; MRX does not name an individual underwriter.
- All pitches are compliant with the FTC endorsement guides (16 CFR Part 255) — disclosed sponsorship, no deceptive claims, no fake reviews.
