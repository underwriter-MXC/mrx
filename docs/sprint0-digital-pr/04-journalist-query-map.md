## What this map is

A query category is the kind of question a reporter or producer asks a source for. Each category below is one MRX can answer **without crossing compliance lines** — that is, without inventing a number, naming a competitor, promising a value, or offering individualized advice.

Each entry includes:

- **The category** in the reporter's language (the kind of question they ask)
- **The MRX angle** (the educational source we can offer)
- **Compliance guardrails** (rules 1-5 + G-04 + G-07 + G-08)
- **First-pitch template pointer** (which file in `05-pitch-templates.md` to use)
- **Tier(s) of outlet** likely to ask this (from `03-outreach-list.md`)

## Category 1 — "What is a mineral right?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "We're writing a primer on what mineral rights are / how royalties work / how mineral ownership works."
- **MRX angle:** MRX editorial team can speak to the basic vocabulary: mineral interest, royalty interest, working interest, NRI, division order, lease, severance tax, the difference between owning minerals and owning surface rights. Source: `src/content/pages/methodology.mdx`, `/learning-center/`.
- **Compliance guardrails:** Educational only. No reference to MRX as a buyer. No "trusted by" claim. Cite public sources (EIA, USGS, state geological surveys).
- **Pitch template:** T-1 in `05-pitch-templates.md`.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1 (personal-finance), Tier 2 (energy trade — for industry-side explainer pieces).

## Category 2 — "I inherited mineral rights. What do I do now?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "Reader just inherited minerals and doesn't know what to do. Can an expert walk them through the first 30 days?"
- **MRX angle:** MRX can speak to the documentation checklist (mineral deed, probate records, division order, recent royalty checks, last lease). Source: `src/content/posts/what-documents-do-you-need-to-sell-mineral-rights-in-texas.mdx` and the inherited-rights pillar.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No "we will buy your minerals"; no "call us for a free offer"; no "act now." The MRX angle is **educational checklist**, not buyer promotion. Cite the official probate court of the relevant state.
- **Pitch template:** T-2 in `05-pitch-templates.md`.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1 (personal-finance), Tier 3 (inheritance / estate / probate), Tier 4 (regional).

## Category 3 — "How do you compare mineral-rights offers?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "Two buyers gave me different offers. How do I tell which is fair?"
- **MRX angle:** MRX can speak to the structural differences (per-acre bonus, NRI retained, retained overrides, post-signing clawback, lease vs. sale), and to the due-diligence questions an owner should ask any buyer (who else sees the offer, who pays transfer taxes, is title curative work included, what is the escrow timeline).
- **Compliance guardrails:** Educational. No named competitor. No "we will beat any offer." No individualized valuation. Source: the methodology page and the offer-review pillar.
- **Pitch template:** T-3 in `05-pitch-templates.md`.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 4.

## Category 4 — "Royalty decline / production decline"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "My royalty checks are smaller every month. Why?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational commentary on decline curves, well-spacing, parent/child well pressure communication, secondary recovery, and operator-specific reporting differences. **Not** engineering advice. The MRX angle is "what to ask your operator and what to read in your own division-order statements," not "you have X recoverable reserves."
- **Compliance guardrails:** No reserve estimates. No engineering claims. Educational only. Cite state geological surveys and EIA where possible.
- **Pitch template:** T-4 in `05-pitch-templates.md`.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1 (consumer), Tier 2 (industry), Tier 4 (regional, especially Permian / Bakken / Eagle Ford).

## Category 5 — "What does a mineral-rights buyer actually do?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "How does the mineral-rights acquisition industry work? Who is buying, and why?"
- **MRX angle:** Commentary on industry structure (public aggregators, private buyers, family offices, operator-affiliated). Educational. MRX does **not** position itself as a typical buyer in this kind of piece; the value of being cited is the educational voice.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No superlative framing. No "we are different." Cite industry sources (NARO publications, public aggregator 10-Ks).
- **Pitch template:** T-5 in `05-pitch-templates.md`.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 7 (creator editorial).

## Category 6 — "How do I title-check my minerals?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "I just inherited. How do I confirm I actually own what I think I own?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational on county recorder, county appraisal district, state land office (TX), probate court, division-order review. **Not** legal advice; not title insurance.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No "we advise," no "our legal opinion." Recommend the owner consult a licensed attorney in their state. Cite the relevant state office.
- **Pitch template:** Reuse T-2 (inheritance) framing with the title-check sub-angle.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1, Tier 3, Tier 4.

## Category 7 — "What is the right time to sell?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "Is now a good time to sell mineral rights?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational on the personal-decision factors (liquidity need, portfolio concentration, family situation, tax posture). The "right time" answer is owner-specific; MRX's contribution is the framework, not the conclusion.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No "sell now." No individualized advice. Educational framework only.
- **Pitch template:** Reuse T-3 (offer comparison) framing with sell/hold sub-angle.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1.

## Category 8 — "How does the royalty owner get paid? What taxes apply?"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "I get a 1099 from the operator. What do I owe?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational on royalty income reporting categories (ordinary income vs. capital gain, depletion allowance basics, severance tax basics, 1031 exchange as a concept). **Not** tax advice.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No "we recommend that you …" No "for tax purposes, you should …" Recommend the owner consult a CPA. Cite IRS publications and state tax authorities.
- **Pitch template:** No dedicated template; reuse T-1 (educational primer) framing with a tax sub-angle.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1 (Bankrate, NerdWallet, Forbes Advisor), Tier 4 (regional).

## Category 9 — "Buyer scams / predatory tactics in mineral acquisition"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "There are bad actors in the mineral-rights space. What should owners watch for?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational on common red flags (lease assignment without explanation, post-signing clawback, undisclosed overrides, signed blank deeds, time-pressure tactics, "we will beat any offer" framing). Educational; not a sales pitch.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No named competitor. No false claims. Cite state attorney-general consumer-protection pages and the Texas General Land Office (where applicable).
- **Pitch template:** Reuse T-3 (offer comparison) framing with the red-flag sub-angle.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1, Tier 3, Tier 4.

## Category 10 — "Estate planning with mineral rights"

- **Reporter phrasing:** "How should mineral rights be handled in a will or trust?"
- **MRX angle:** Educational on the existence of mineral interest as an asset class, the role of mineral-rights-specific clauses in wills/trusts, and the importance of working with an estate attorney. **Not** legal advice.
- **Compliance guardrails:** No legal advice. No "we recommend." Recommend an estate attorney. Cite NAIFA / bar publications for the framework.
- **Pitch template:** Reuse T-2 (inheritance) framing with the estate-planning sub-angle.
- **Outlet tiers:** Tier 1, Tier 3.
