## Authoritative source plan

1. [Railroad Commission of Texas, Online Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) identifies available query systems and states that online data sets are informational, continually updated, and not authoritative public records with legal force.
2. [Railroad Commission of Texas, Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) identifies record-search fields including county, drilling date, lease or well name and number, operator, field, Commission identifiers, survey, abstract, section, and block. It also states that the Commission lacks authority over private lease, royalty, and property-right matters.
3. [Railroad Commission of Texas, Public GIS Viewer](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/gis-viewer/) supports map-based identifier research while expressly limiting legal, engineering, surveying, boundary, accuracy, completeness, and currency use.
4. [Texas General Land Office, Land Grant Database](https://www.glo.texas.gov/archives-heritage/search-our-collections/land-grant-search) identifies searchable historical land-grant fields. A result remains an archive lead and is not present-title proof.
5. [Robertson County, County Clerk](https://www.co.robertson.tx.us/page/robertson.County.Clerk) identifies the current county clerk office and links to the county-selected Texas Land Records destination. The article does not treat an index result as a complete instrument or title conclusion.
6. [Texas Property Code, Chapter 12](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/pdf/PR.12.pdf) supplies bounded recording context only. The article does not interpret a particular instrument or give owner-specific legal advice.

No source establishes private title, present ownership, acreage, payment rights, lease meaning, deed effect, mineral value, production potential, offer quality, or a transaction decision.
