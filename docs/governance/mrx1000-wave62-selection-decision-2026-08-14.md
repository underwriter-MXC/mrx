## Search job and fail-closed boundary

Wave 62 owns one narrow job: show how to divide a written total offer by the confirmed net mineral acres covered by that same offer, label every example as hypothetical, and stop when the offer amount, acreage, interest type, property scope, or consideration components do not align.

It may:

- define the worksheet inputs and arithmetic operation;
- show clearly labeled hypothetical examples;
- distinguish gross acres, stated ownership fraction, and stated NMA only far enough to validate the denominator;
- show how an unchanged total produces a different unit figure when the denominator changes;
- explain when an aggregate calculation is possible and why it cannot allocate value among tracts; and
- route adjacent title, definitions, comparability, buyer-review, and contract questions to their owning articles or qualified professionals.

It does not:

- explain what market, geology, production, lease, operator, location, or timing factors make mineral rights more or less valuable;
- estimate, certify, or imply fair market value or a fair price-per-NMA range;
- state that price per NMA alone makes two offers, tracts, buyers, or transactions comparable;
- rank buyers or offers or advise an owner to accept, reject, counter, sell, hold, or sign;
- interpret a deed, division order, ownership schedule, offer, purchase agreement, title finding, or legal description;
- establish ownership, acreage, title, royalty burden, future development, future production, or future payments; or
- provide legal, tax, appraisal, engineering, geology, estate, investment, suitability, or financial-planning advice.
