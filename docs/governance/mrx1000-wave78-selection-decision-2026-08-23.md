## Mandatory cross-check fields

The workflow records only the displayed identity and connection fields needed to compare one record:

- source system, retrieval date, and record status;
- API number, permit or status number, well number, operator, lease or unit name/number, county, field, and survey/section reference when displayed;
- wellbore profile, formation or depth interval, filing purpose, and relevant submitted, approved, completion, or production dates when displayed;
- the owner record used for comparison, its displayed date or version, and the exact field compared;
- one result per field: `match`, `mismatch`, or `unverified`; and
- one neutral clarification question for every mismatch or unverified field.
