## Authoritative source plan

1. [Andrews County Online Official Public Records Search](https://www.co.andrews.tx.us/322/Online-Official-Public-Records-Search) supplies the official starting route, Tyler portal link, normal posting note, and county-stated search limitations.
2. [Andrews County Clerk](https://www.co.andrews.tx.us/181/County-Clerk) identifies the responsible office and record classes while preserving access restrictions.
3. [Andrews County Appraisal District](https://andrewscad.org/) supplies the official district role and official property-search route.
4. [Andrews CAD Property Search](https://esearch.andrewscad.org/) supplies current search fields and research-use limitations without creating title or valuation proof.
5. [Texas General Land Office Land Grant Search](https://www.glo.texas.gov/archives-heritage/search-our-collections/land-grant-search) supplies historical archive fields only.
6. [Railroad Commission Research Queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) supplies the official regulatory-system directory and informational-data boundary.
7. [Railroad Commission Public GIS Viewer](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/gis-viewer/) supplies a separate approximate map locator with no legal force.
8. [Railroad Commission Oil and Gas Well Records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) supplies bounded record-request fields without proving a property connection.
