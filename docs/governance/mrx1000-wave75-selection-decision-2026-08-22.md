## Mandatory cannibalization and compliance boundaries

This article owns only statement-level provenance notation for an AI-assisted review. It may not:

- compare providers, buyers, offers, acquisition services, technologies, or platforms;
- describe MRX differentiation, proprietary capabilities, performance, accuracy, reliability, competitive advantage, or review quality;
- calculate value, price per acre, production, royalty, ownership, title, legal, tax, contract, payment, or transaction conclusions;
- promise that a source trace makes AI output accurate, complete, reliable, unbiased, current, safe, auditable, certified, or less risky;
- treat a citation, URL, document name, access date, or human-review mark as proof that the underlying statement is correct;
- repeat the document-package index, review-intake checklist, seller-upload, valuation-input register, source-and-input register, offer comparison, or platform-differentiation jobs;
- turn an unsupported, conflicting, ambiguous, or unclear statement into a fact; or
- make a recommendation to sell, hold, sign, transfer, negotiate, rely on an output, or choose a provider.

Unsupported, conflicting, or unclear output remains an `unresolved inference` until a qualified human checks the underlying evidence. The label is a workflow status, not a factual or legal conclusion.
