## Search job and boundaries

Wave 60 owns one narrow job: reconcile an appraisal-district mineral tax value with a voluntary directional sale estimate by matching the exact interest or account scope, effective date, evidence cutoff, purpose, data identity, assumptions, rights, and exclusions.

The permitted output is limited to:

- `matched`;
- `not comparable`;
- `conflicted`; or
- `professional review needed`.

It does not:

- re-teach broad value drivers, a full valuation workflow, or range-width mechanics;
- calculate value, a tax amount, expected owner net, or a sale price;
- decide which number is correct, fair, higher, or lower;
- set or interpret protest rights, notices, procedures, or deadlines;
- apply a property-tax formula to a voluntary sale estimate;
- infer title, ownership, reserves, future development, or future production; or
- treat either number as a floor, ceiling, appraisal conclusion, or offer.

Owner-specific appraisal, engineering, geology, title, legal, protest, and tax questions are routed to the applicable appraisal district and qualified professionals.
