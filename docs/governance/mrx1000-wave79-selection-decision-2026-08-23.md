## Mandatory log fields

Each entry preserves only the record identity and displayed-name evidence needed to reproduce the observation:

- source system or owner-record type;
- retrieval date and displayed record date or status when shown;
- exact displayed record identifier;
- exact operator or payor name as displayed;
- owner-record reference when one is being compared;
- one outcome label: `same displayed name`, `different displayed name`, or `unavailable`; and
- one neutral clarification question for every different or unavailable result.
