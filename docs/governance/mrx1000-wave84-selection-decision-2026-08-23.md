## Required register fields

The blank register is limited to:

- register record ID and record status;
- source-stated event type;
- well name, API/UWI, or other source-provided identifier;
- source document or channel;
- publisher, custodian, or originating party;
- source publication date and retrieval date;
- claimed event date or date range, if expressly stated;
- operator name exactly as shown;
- property-connection status;
- observed post-claim reporting periods labeled non-causal;
- exact source excerpt and stable locator;
- unknowns, discrepancies, and conflicting-source references; and
- follow-up owner, requested record, and next action date.

`Event claim` means wording copied from an identified source. It is not a finding that an intervention occurred, affected the named property, caused a production change, or created value.

## Authoritative source plan

1. [Texas Railroad Commission oil and gas well records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) supports identifying well-file channels, completion or recompletion records, and request identifiers without interpreting a filing.
2. [Texas Railroad Commission online well records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records-online/) supports preserving the PDF file, record profile, page, well or lease identifier, and retrieval date.
3. [Texas Railroad Commission oil and gas forms](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/oil-and-gas-forms/) supports recording the exact form name and revision shown, including forms whose titles reference completion or recompletion, without deciding what the filing proves.
4. [Texas Railroad Commission production data](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/production-data/) supports logging dated reported periods as non-causal observations without attributing a change to an event.
5. [SEC filing search](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings) supports preserving a public-company filing identity, filer, form, filing date, accession reference, and exact passage locator without treating a filing as property-specific event confirmation.

A first-party operator release, presentation, operating update, owner-relations response, or owner-provided document may be logged as its own source class. The register preserves its identity and wording but does not validate or interpret it.
