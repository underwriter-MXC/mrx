## Required inventory fields

The blank worksheet is limited to:

- tract or interest identifier;
- claimed status;
- as-of date;
- evidence document;
- payer or operator;
- last payment or production month;
- unknown field; and
- follow-up owner.

`Claimed status` means an unverified label copied from an owner-provided or located record. It is not a conclusion about ownership, lease status, operations, payment entitlement, or value.

## Authoritative source plan

1. [Texas Railroad Commission production data](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/production-data/) supports identifying the official production-query channel and recording the query date and located lease or well reference without treating the result as ownership, payment, status, or value proof.
2. [Texas Railroad Commission Public GIS Viewer](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/gis-viewer/) supports locating map, survey, lease, API, and well identifiers while preserving the Commission's warning that GIS information has no legal force or effect.
3. [Texas Railroad Commission oil and gas well records](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records/) supports logging the official record location and request identifiers without interpreting a well file.
4. [Texas General Land Office energy mapping resources](https://www.glo.texas.gov/energy) supports locating state-land and state-lease references when applicable, without construing a state lease or ownership interest.
5. [Texas Unclaimed Property](https://www.claimittexas.gov/) supports recording a dated owner-name search and claim reference, if any, without treating a search result as proof of mineral ownership, suspense status, or payment entitlement.

The inventory may also record a first-party payer or operator statement, portal document, owner-relations response, or revenue record already in the owner's file. It does not interpret that material.
