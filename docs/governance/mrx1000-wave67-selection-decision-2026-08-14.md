## Exclusive content job

This article assumes a family has already decided to receive mineral-rights inquiries. It teaches the family how to choose one administrative point person who receives messages, confirms the requested recipient and subject, preserves the original communication, routes the same information to the right people, gathers questions, and sends only family-approved administrative replies.

The role is a communication convenience only. It creates no ownership, legal authority, consent, agency, fiduciary status, signing power, or decision right.

It may provide selection criteria, a narrow role description, an intake-and-routing protocol, a handoff rule, an out-of-office backup, an opening response, and a reset procedure when the contact exceeds scope.

It must not:

- revisit whether the family should receive inquiries, align on a sale, or resolve disagreement; Article 146 owns the pre-decision family conversation;
- call the point person an agent, representative, attorney-in-fact, fiduciary, authorized signer, or decision-maker;
- create or interpret a power of attorney, trust, entity, estate, brokerage, listing, agency, or other legally operative relationship;
- determine ownership, authority, consent, signature, title, probate, tax, valuation, offer fairness, or transaction terms;
- teach the selling process, timeline, offer comparison, negotiation, mistake, trap, decision-log, document-package, partial-sale, or post-sale jobs;
- give buyer, operator, broker, landman, attorney, tax, valuation, or other provider recommendations; or
- substitute family shorthand for review of the actual documents by qualified professionals.
