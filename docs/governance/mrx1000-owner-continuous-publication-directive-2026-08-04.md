## Decision

MRX1000 articles are not subject to staged numerical publication caps or elapsed-time scale gates. Article count is not a publication gate. An otherwise releasable article must not be held because the corpus has reached 10, 25, 50, or another batch count; because a 7-, 14-, 30-, or 60-day observation period has not elapsed; because an indexing-percentage observation is pending; or because a numerical cap-lift decision ID is absent.

This decision supersedes the following only where they impose those numerical or elapsed-time restrictions:

- D-2026-0720-11;
- D-2026-0721-21 sections 6.1 and 6.2;
- D-2026-0801-10's prohibition on a 26th MRX1000 article;
- D-2026-0804-15's cap-based HOLD; and
- derivative reports, scripts, tests, and agent instructions.

The MRX1000 program target remains 1,000 articles. That program scope is not a staged release ceiling and does not require 10-to-25 or 25-to-50 promotion decisions.

## Gates preserved

Publication remains fail closed for article-specific defects. Every article must still have the required evidence for:

1. editorial quality and SEO/AEO usefulness;
2. factual and citation support;
3. compliance and publication-risk review;
4. unique, article-specific exact-title hero/share creative;
5. canonical, Open Graph, Twitter/X, schema, image, and accessibility identity;
6. successful tests and production builds;
7. rollback readiness; and
8. post-deployment live HTTP and rendered verification on every active production target.

Quality evidence controls release eligibility. Article count and elapsed time do not.
