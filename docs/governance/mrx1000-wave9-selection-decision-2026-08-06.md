## Selection rationale and overlap boundaries

- The two valuation rows perform different jobs. The comprehensive guide is an owner-facing assessment workbook that separates ownership, production, lease, market, and scenario evidence. The Texas-specific guide shows how to reconcile county records, Railroad Commission data, operator records, and offer assumptions without presenting a universal price formula.
- The three offer-safety rows separate counterparty diligence, suspected fraud response, and preparation for comparing legitimate offers before a review call. None may label a party predatory or fraudulent without evidence, promise a fair price, or substitute for legal review.
- The estate-planning row focuses on ongoing administration, authority, record custody, and successor instructions. The inheritance row focuses on the post-death ownership chain, probate or non-probate evidence, payor updates, and basis records.
- The Section 1031 row addresses tax consequences, reporting, cash or non-like-kind property, basis carryover, and related-party issues. It does not duplicate Wave 8's eligibility, mechanics, sale-versus-exchange, or federal sale-reporting articles.
- The royalty-check row is a gross-to-net reconciliation guide focused on the ownership decimal, product volumes, price, taxes, deductions, and suspense adjustments. It does not treat the statement as a title opinion or duplicate the existing trend-analysis article.
- The MRX process comparison describes differences in role, workflow, compensation, market exposure, and deliverables. It may not claim superiority, independence, guaranteed pricing, broker licensure, an appraisal, or a conflict-free process.
- All ten inherited drafts contain duplicated or unsupported material and must be rewritten from verified primary sources rather than lightly edited.
