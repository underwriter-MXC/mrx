## Selection conditions

1. The general lease guide and held-by-production guide must perform distinct search jobs, cross-link to one another, and use separate creative assets. The held-by-production guide must not decide whether an owner-specific lease remains effective.
2. The inheritance-before-sale guide focuses on authority, title records, ownership scope, co-owners, tax records, and sale readiness. The inherited-royalty-check guide focuses on payee transfer, operator records, suspense, division orders, and statement reconciliation.
3. The capital-gains guide is general tax education only. It must cite current IRS primary sources, distinguish a sale from royalty income, avoid a universal tax classification or rate, and direct owner-specific questions to a qualified tax professional.
4. The underwriter-review guides must disclose that MRX provides a directional review and may become a buyer. They must not describe MRX as independent when that conflict exists or promise a free review, a guaranteed value, the highest offer, a result, or a timetable.
5. Every article requires a unique 1200-by-630 WebP with the exact canonical title visibly rendered in the pixels and bound as the page hero, Open Graph image, Twitter/X image, Article schema image, and CMS share asset.
6. Publication requires complete-file hash-locked editorial, factual/citation, and compliance reviews; current source-access evidence; all repository gates and tests; production deployment; and page-by-page live assertions on every active production target.
