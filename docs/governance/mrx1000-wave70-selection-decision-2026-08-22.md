## Mandatory cannibalization boundaries

This article may not:

- explain valuation methods or teach an owner to calculate value;
- validate, reperform, revise, or recalculate a valuation;
- assess source reliability or decide whether a requested replacement is correct;
- duplicate the sale document-package inventory or the sale decision log;
- determine title, ownership, acreage, decimal interest, property identity, legal effect, or document sufficiency;
- perform scenario or sensitivity analysis;
- present an appraisal, certified value, fair-value conclusion, offer-fairness conclusion, buyer ranking, negotiation advice, or sell-or-hold recommendation; or
- imply that an intake status means a valuation input, range, or conclusion changed.

Allowed intake statuses are limited to `received`, `source linked`, `clarification requested`, `accepted for review`, `not accepted for review`, and `unresolved`. `Accepted for review` means only that the request may be considered. `Not accepted for review` is not a substantive determination.
