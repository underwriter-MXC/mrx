## Search job and boundaries

Wave 61 owns one narrow job: construct a neutral sell-versus-keep decision record that aligns the exact rights, current royalty and operating evidence, dated assumptions, complete transaction terms, owner purpose, alternatives, reversibility, stop conditions, and professional-review questions.

The permitted process outcomes are limited to:

- `ready to compare`;
- `ready to explore an alternative`;
- `pause for evidence`; or
- `professional review needed`.

It does not:

- re-teach mineral-rights definitions or beginner orientation;
- produce or certify an owner-specific value, reserves case, commodity forecast, expected net, tax result, or suitability conclusion;
- decide whether the owner should sell, keep, sell a portion, accept, reject, or sign;
- treat a royalty check, public production record, estimate, benchmark, or proposal as a complete decision;
- infer title, ownership, acreage, decimals, lease meaning, future development, future production, future prices, or future payments;
- compare a headline amount without the complete rights and transaction terms; or
- replace qualified title, legal, tax, appraisal, engineering, geology, estate, financial-planning, or transaction review.

The decision remains the owner’s. Owner-specific professional questions are routed precisely rather than converted into unsupported advice.
