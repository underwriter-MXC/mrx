The article preserves these boundaries:

- GLO land-grant records can support historical property research but do not establish the later county-level chain of title.
- County deed, lease, probate, trust, division-order, royalty, and correspondence records answer different questions; none is silently elevated into a title opinion.
- RRC well, production, permit, and GIS records provide regulatory identity and operating context, not ownership, payment, authoritative survey boundaries, future drilling certainty, or owner-specific value.
- A Texas unclaimed-property result is an investigation lead, not complete proof of the mineral interest or the reason funds were reported.
- An initial evaluation may produce a missing-evidence plan instead of a numerical conclusion.
