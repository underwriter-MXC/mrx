## Search job and differentiation

Wave 56 owns the **property-matched local pricing-range assembly and reporting** job. It does not own a general value definition, a pricing-factor catalog, a single-comparable relevance screen, a valuation-method explanation, an offer-fairness review, or a title/contract conclusion.

The page must:

1. explain why no universal county, basin, or statewide price-per-acre range applies to every interest;
2. define the relevant area from the subject tract, depths, formation, and development relationship outward;
3. separate producing, nonproducing, leased, unleased, and different-interest cohorts;
4. preserve written subject-property evidence, candidate transactions, public operating context, and property-specific income evidence in separate lanes;
5. normalize only verified consideration and acreage or revenue units;
6. report an evidence envelope with cohort, exclusions, sensitivities, confidence, limitations, and reset triggers; and
7. state when the evidence is too thin or mismatched to support any typical local range.

Nearest-page boundaries:

- `comparable-mineral-sales-what-makes-a-transaction-relevant` remains the test for whether one candidate transaction is relevant.
- `key-factors-that-determine-your-mineral-rights-assessment-pricing-range` remains the factor and mechanism catalog.
- `understanding-valuation-methodology-how-it-affects-your-mineral-rights-final-price` remains the methodology and assumption-effects guide.
- `how-to-know-if-your-mineral-rights-offer-is-fair` remains the complete-offer fairness, document, buyer, and closing review.
- Wave 56 uniquely assembles screened evidence into a dated local envelope or records that no range is supportable yet.

## Evidence boundary

Current RRC sources may support dated reported Texas production, operating, query-path, lag, and correction context. Current EIA sources may support dated public commodity-price context. Current Texas GLO materials may support public state mineral-lease sale context but not a private fee-mineral or royalty sale price. The Texas Comptroller manual may support income-discounting method context for its statutory property-tax purpose, not a private transaction appraisal. MRX methodology and selling-options pages may support directional-review scope and owner-option categories. None of these sources can prove owner-specific title, ownership, reserves, future development, realized price, value, legal effect, tax result, offer, expected net, suitable decision, sale, or closing.
