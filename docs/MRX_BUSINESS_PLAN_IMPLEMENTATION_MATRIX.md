## Release interpretation

Supabase is authoritative for identity, private files, owner memory, facts, consent, and the canonical transcript. GoHighLevel is the CRM, scheduling, communication, GHL Voice AI, and operational conversation layer. Original document binaries and raw OCR stay in encrypted Supabase storage; GHL receives communication text plus redacted document summaries/status/approved facts only. MRX AI Guides are fictional educational interfaces and are never represented as licensed professionals or article authors. The public website remains nationwide, while Texas receives the deepest initial commercial and content focus.
