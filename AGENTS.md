## Exact-title hero and share image

Finalize and lock the exact article title before image work.

Create one unique 1200x630 WebP hero image that:

- Renders the exact finalized title in visible pixels with identical wording, spelling, capitalization, and punctuation
- Is unique to the article and legible at practical desktop, mobile, and social-preview sizes
- Uses an article-specific evidence scene and contains no misleading fabricated evidence
- Passes visual, OCR, crop, contrast, accessibility, MIME, dimension, filename, and SHA checks

Use that same canonical asset and exact bytes for the visible hero, CMS featured/share field, `og:image`, `twitter:image`, Article schema image, and every equivalent share field. Do not substitute alternate crops, regenerated variants, visually similar files, or metadata-only replacements. Production byte and SHA assertions must prove the references are identical.

## Materially distinct in-body image

Create one separate 1200x675 WebP in-body image that:

- Renders the exact approved keyword phrase in visible pixels with identical wording, spelling, capitalization, and punctuation
- Serves a specific explanatory or evidentiary function in the article
- Uses a materially different composition from the hero
- Uses a materially different camera angle
- Uses a materially different subject arrangement
- Depicts a materially different evidence scene
- Passes visual, OCR, crop, contrast, accessibility, MIME, dimension, filename, and SHA checks

The in-body image must not be a regenerated variant, alternate crop, resized derivative, recolored copy, prompt neighbor, or lightly rearranged version of the hero. Different text alone is not sufficient. Similarity in topic is allowed only when the visual construction and evidence function are independently distinct.

## Mandatory quality and release gates

An article is not complete until every applicable gate passes and its evidence is recorded.

1. **Source:** authoritative, relevant, retrievable sources support material claims; freshness and claim-to-source mapping are verified; unsupported legal, tax, financial, regulatory, title, production, price, percentage, deadline, or money claims are removed or corrected.
2. **Originality and cannibalization:** the finalized job-to-be-done, audience, intent, evidence, and outcome materially differ from the repository, live site, and already-owned program corpus.
3. **Editorial, SEO, and AEO:** the article answers the finalized job, uses coherent title/slug/headings/direct-answer/FAQ/internal-link structure, avoids filler and keyword stuffing, and is useful to people and answer engines.
4. **Compliance:** required legal, financial, tax, title, valuation, privacy, consent, buyer-interest, and service-boundary disclosures are accurate; no promise, appraisal claim, pressure, or misrepresentation is present.
5. **Copy:** the final copy is original, specific, accurate, readable, grammatically clean, and free of placeholders, planning residue, fabricated quotations, unsupported statistics, duplicated sections, or incomplete notes.
6. **Accessibility:** heading structure, link text, alt text, keyboard use, contrast, legibility, and responsive behavior pass.
7. **Metadata and schema:** canonical URL, title, description, Open Graph, Twitter/X, Article, Breadcrumb, and FAQ data are syntactically valid and consistent with visible content; all hero/share/schema references resolve to the same exact asset.
8. **Images:** the exact-title 1200x630 hero and materially distinct exact-keyword 1200x675 in-body asset pass full-resolution and scaled visual review, OCR, alt, path, MIME, dimensions, byte size, SHA, and production-reference checks.
9. **Build and tests:** required formatting, lint, type, content, heading, grammar, link, schema, compliance, route, asset, unit, regression, and production-build checks pass; failures are repaired and rerun, not waived.
10. **Rollback:** record the pre-release state, rollback reference, and practical recovery procedure before deployment.
11. **Commit and push:** the release commit contains only intended article, asset, evidence, ledger, and directly required supporting changes; verify the remote contains the intended commit.
12. **Deploy:** deploy only from the verified isolated worktree, expected branch, and sole release lane; verify the provider, project, deployment ID, deployment URL, inspect URL, status, and promoted aliases.
13. **Public apex and `www`:** require fresh public HTTP behavior, correct redirect/canonicalization, exact title and H1, indexability, visible content, sources, metadata, schema, analytics, disclosures, links, and both images. A Ready origin or deployment dashboard alone is not publication proof.
14. **Image bytes and SHA:** retrieve production-served hero and in-body bytes; verify HTTP status, MIME, dimensions, byte size, cryptographic SHA, canonical hero reuse, and a distinct in-body SHA.
15. **Chrome visual:** inspect the live article in extension-backed Google Chrome at desktop and mobile-responsive sizes; verify title/keyword pixels, legibility, crop, composition, layout, overflow, and absence of clipping, overlap, garbling, or placeholders.
16. **Ledger reconciliation:** record article number, finalized identity, job-to-be-done, paths, source evidence, image identities and SHAs, commit, deployment, public URL, verification results, rollback reference, and state transitions in the canonical MRX1000 ledger.

## Failure recovery and completion evidence

Recover ordinary failures automatically. Retry transient service/network failures, regenerate noncompliant included-quota images, correct copy/metadata/schema/link/test/build failures, repair safe worktree or branch alignment, rerun deployment or verification, and roll back/redeploy when needed. Do not turn an ordinary failure into an owner approval gate.

Staged drafts, manifests, task descriptions, generated files, local previews, deployment readiness, and specialist reports are inputs only. A specialist's claim or self-attestation is not verification. Completion requires independent verification by Codex or another designated verifier under the standing MRX routing rule.

An article may be reported complete only when its identity is finalized, every mandatory gate passes, the intended commits are pushed, production is deployed, public apex/`www` behavior is verified, production image bytes/dimensions/SHAs are exact, Chrome visual verification passes, and the canonical ledger is reconciled.
